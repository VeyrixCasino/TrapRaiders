# 13 — Ketamine-type dissociatives (Kit K)

Game catalog feel cards. **No recipes. No doses. No glassware.** Street rumor + forum consensus for NPC talk and Flask SKUs.

Scope: 2-oxo arylcyclohexylamines that play as *ketamine-likes* — ortho-halogen / ortho-MeO / 2-thienyl, short-to-mid hole fantasy. FXE and HXE sit chemically on the 3-sub 2-oxo-PCE side; Kit K still owns them. **Do not steal MXE / O-PCE / PCP family SKUs** (other assignments).

Scores are independent 0–100 or `null`. Lead axes: **enjoyment, rush, euphoria, compulsiveness (hole-fiending)**, then negatives. Extra axes when evidence exists: **dissociation_depth, warmth, mania**.

## Headliners

### ketamine — Kitchen-door hole. Bump until the floor wins.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 84 | 42 | 74 | 86 | 62 | 90 | 54 | 16 |

The hole. Short. Party / clinic / kitchen floor. Mid bumps are giggles, music, and “I love this couch.” Then the fiend: another key, another key, until speech is soup and the room is a television you fell into. Rush is a warm whoosh, not a stim snap — still the most *immediate* IN punch in this kit. Euphoria is real (float, afterglow, dumb joy) and not MDMA. Compulsiveness is the family legend. Negatives: bladder career, K-cramps, falls, amnesia, next-day fog. Enantiomers (S more disso, R more “weird”) are the same bottle on the street. Norketamine is a metabolite, not a SKU.

### DCK — DCK is ket that stays for dinner.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 78 | 24 | 72 | 70 | 52 | 72 | 60 | 20 |

Ket minus the ring chlorine. Longer, cleaner, more *do a thing* at low, still a hole if you insist. Forum love: physical + cognitive euphoria even when you can still watch a movie. Rush is late and polite. Hole is shallower than ket for a lot of people and then overstays — 15–20 minute holes on a multi-hour plateau show up in old threads. Compulsive, but the duration kills the bump-loop and replaces it with a *weekend* loop. Immune/antibacterial rumor is forum folklore; bladder still in the room, maybe less gram-for-gram because people use less. DXE sticker ≠ MXE.

### 2F-DCK — 2F-DCK batch lottery.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 58 | 32 | 56 | 76 | 58 | 70 | 42 | 14 |

Ket’s fluoro cousin and the envelope you gamble on. Good shards: most people would fail a blind test vs ket, hole a little less deep, linger a little longer, visuals darker. Bad shards: sulfur smell, glass that does nothing, “cringy,” 500 mg of sand. Forum “R/S isomer” talk is almost always **impurity / leftover solvent**, not a chiral boutique. Enjoyment is the average of shine and trash — that is why it scores under ket, not because the molecule is cursed. Same hole-fiend as ket when the bag is real. Something is often *missing* (the hug, the defined visuals). Bladder still collects.

### NENK — First-wave leftover. Can’t hole, can disappoint.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 38 | 16 | 44 | 48 | 50 | 32 | 48 | 12 |

Old ethketamine. Ket-heads called it meh; ket-naive called it fine. Mild, short, warm-ish, ceiling. Bluelight consensus: basically impossible to hole, redose does less than you want, IN wrecks the nose (bleed, burn). A few reports of float + “dissociative opiate” body. Do not file this as O-PCE. First-wave RC leftover, dusty Flask sticker.

### 2F-NENDCK / CanKet — Ket’s longer cousin who drank a coffee and forgot the hug.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 66 | 36 | 52 | 72 | 64 | 66 | 38 | 30 |

Canberra’s “new ket,” then the world’s. Same carbon count as FXE, fluorine at **2** not 3. Sold as ket, FXE, MXE, 2F-DCK, once in a while as itself. Feel split: some say more recreational than 2F-DCK (stim edge, visuals, functional, physical buzz); harm-redox writeups say mild-to-no euphoria, more disorient, more hangover, 4–6 h that is *def not ketamine*. NDEWS chatter: longer/stronger ket **or** body-horror visuals and bladder/kidney scare. Compulsive because it is the current ket-shaped powder. Mania is a tinge, not 3-MeO-PCP. Street name soup lives here.

### FXE — They said it was MXE. Labs said CanKet.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 61 | 30 | 60 | 55 | 48 | 62 | 58 | 28 |

True **3-FXE** (fluorexetamine): between ket and MXE on paper — a bit stimmer than ket, visuals, mixed warmth. Early 2023 “warm FXE” vs later “cold FXE” batch myth is now mostly explained by **you were not holding 3-FXE**. DrugsData: every pre-standard “FXE” sample was 2-FXE/CanKet. Score the *molecule* mid; treat any NPC bag stamped FXE as identity-uncertain and probably CanKet. If a future lab-confirmed 3-FXE brick shows up, this row can move. Do not print a third bottle called “fluorexetamine” next to FXE.

### HXE — Couch, not a hole. Warm weak dinner guest.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 67 | 14 | 60 | 42 | 36 | 40 | 74 | 18 |

3-hydroxy MXE cousin that Kit K kept. Dreamy, introspective, light-body, music. People who like it call it comforting — 3-HO-PCP-adjacent warmth without the god-mode. PsychonautWiki is slightly colder (“less warmth than MXE, empty/rough at times”); Reddit is hotter. Both agree: **hole power is missing**. You mix it or you sit. Rush is a shrug. Compulsiveness is real but lazy. Best Flask “I want a hug, not a kidnapping” bottle. Chemically MXE-family; still this assignment.

### 2-MeO-ketamine — Ket’s methoxy cousin who never showed up.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 28 | 10 | 26 | 22 | 34 | 30 | 28 | 10 |

Not MXE. Ortho-methoxy, N-methyl, first-wave RC. Forum verdict: ragged, less interesting, less euphoric, needs a pile, still doesn’t go anywhere. A couple of people enjoyed it as a mixer or as “cleaner than an MXE binge.” Most of the room walked away. Skip unless the crate is a joke or a history shelf.

### 2-BDCK — Bromo ket. Empty squares, burning nose.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 34 | 28 | 40 | 30 | 46 | 28 | 50 | 8 |

The halogen that lost. Fast in, short stay, a bit of warmth and float, then empty geometry. Weakest of the 2-halo ket series in every thread that actually finished a gram. IN is famously awful. Hole is a rumor you spend a mountain chasing. Fine as a one-off “periodic table” gag SKU. Not a wall.

### tiletamine — Polar-bear ket. Vet shelf that overstays.

| enjoyment | rush | euphoria | compulsiveness | negatives | dissociation_depth | warmth | mania |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 48 | 40 | 58 | 44 | 72 | 78 | 32 | 26 |

2-thienyl, N-ethyl, 2-oxo — ket’s veterinary cousin. CanTEST saw it in “ketamine” bags. Pure tiletamine reports: heavy body-numb bliss, dreamy, long, walk like you are drunk in a lagging game. Telazol/Zoletil adds zolazepam — sudden lights-out, then sudden awake, benzo on the piss test, a handful of published abuse/fatality cases. Enjoyment is split (heavenly body vs “why did I steal from the zoo”). Rush is more of a hit than DCK. Negatives win: duration, motor wreck, mix-up with the benzo combo, freakout flag. Museum / vet-joke crate, not the Friday bottle.

## Not distinct SKUs

- **esketamine / arketamine / Spravato** — ketamine’s hands, not new molecules for the wall.
- **norketamine, hydroxynorketamine, dehydronorketamine** — metabolites.
- **iso-ketamine, meta-ketamine, oMDCK / mMDCK** — paper isomers, no bag culture.
- **Telazol / Zoletil** — combo product (tiletamine + zolazepam), flag on the tiletamine row.
- **tusi / pink cocaine** — marketing color, usually ket ± extras.
- **2-FK, fluoroketamine** — 2F-DCK.
- **street FXE, 2-FXE, CanKet** — see merge table.
