# Duration note 04 — PsychonautWiki + Erowid (MDPV / α-PVP / MDPHP / α-PHP)

Game catalog. **Not a cook book. Not a dose guide.** Clock folklore only.

Pulled **duration tables** and **experience-vault summaries** (how long the smile lasted). No recipes. No milligram how-to. No injection SOP. Route is a clock flag only.

**House rule this file exists to enforce**

Gameplay **Duration (D)** = primary **FUN window** in minutes. Used only in `WeightedDuration`.

| Clock | What it is | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| **Peak / Plateau** | Height of the smile | **D** |
| Come-up | Door into the peak | optional `onset`, not D |
| Offset / coming down | Peak is over; may still be wired | **not D** unless a vault still calls it fun (rare; say so) |
| Total | Come-up + peak + offset, **excluding** after-effects (PW definition) | **not D** |
| After-effects / hangover / residual wake | Heart, insomnia, crash, “can’t sleep it off” | `HalfLife` / C / A — **never D** |

PW: after-effects are **not** part of Total. Erowid prints After Effects and Hangover as their own rows. Do not copy either into D.

**Locks**

- **α-PVP D = 90.** Designer mix. Do not move.
- **MDPV D must be substantially > 90.** A 48 (or any D ≤ 90) is inverted. This note replaces the pending-null.

This file does **not** write `GAMEPLAY_STATS_*`.

## Schema (so the quoted rows mean the same thing)

[PsychonautWiki Duration](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Duration):

| Phase | PW meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total** | First administration → effects wear off into sobriety. After-effects excluded. |
| **Onset** | Until first alerts. |
| **Come up** | First alerts → highest intensity. |
| **Peak** | Intensity at its height. |
| **Offset** | End of peak → sober. “Coming down.” |
| **After effects** | Residual after the experience concludes. Hangover or afterglow. |

Erowid’s MDPV table uses **Coming Up / Plateau / Coming Down** for Come-up / Peak / Offset. Hangover is a separate blank row.

PW’s MDPV / α-PVP / α-PHP sheets often **omit a Come-up row** and print Onset + Peak + Offset + After effects. Where PW has no Come-up, the table below says `—` and Erowid (or vault timing) is the come-up source.

## Experience vault summaries (FUN length, not residual)

Summaries only. No amounts. No how-to.

### MDPV — [exp_MDPV](https://www.erowid.org/experiences/subs/exp_MDPV.shtml)

Large vault (glowing, addiction, health-problem, and train-wreck piles). Clock that keeps repeating:

- **First-hit smile is hours, not a 45-minute bang.** *So Powerful* (exp79796): come-up described as lasting **about two hours**, then the feeling **held**; writer clocked the experience at **about six hours** before taking more. That six is closer to Total than to Peak; the two-hour climb + hold is the FUN.
- **Plateau can feel like a short roll, then a long wired leftover.** *Best Times and Worst Times* (exp88139): come-up ~15 minutes, plateau already on at ~30 minutes, **ecstasy-like smile for roughly an hour and three-quarters**, then a coke-like remainder. Later redoses **stacked time** instead of bringing the smile back. Vault line the writer learned too late: more hits **exponentially add time**. That extra time is mostly ugly wake, not a bigger D.
- **Redose stretches the night; it does not reprint the first glow.** *7 Months on the PeeVee* / *Redosing and Comedown*: later hits raise stimulation and worsen the crash more than they restore pleasure. Erowid’s own effects-page caution matches this.
- **Aftermath is the brand.** Titles like *Up All Night Afterwards*, *When Will It Stop*, *Worst Comedown In A Long Time*. Days-long residue belongs to HalfLife / A, not D.

Read against the tables: Erowid oral **plateau 30–180 min** and PW oral **peak 1–4 h** are the FUN band. Oral **total 2–7 h** and **after-effects 2–48 h** are the leftover.

### α-PVP — [exp_aPVP](https://www.erowid.org/experiences/subs/exp_aPVP.shtml)

Smaller vault. Same split the house already locked:

- *Great High Couple Too Many Side Effects* (exp103367): **main high about three hours**, then a slow wear-off. Elevated heart rate and restlessness **stuck around for hours after the high**. “Side effects … stick around much longer after the high goes away.” That is the D vs HalfLife sentence in vault English.
- *A Completely Misunderstood Compound* (exp99718): one timed sublingual night — euphoria building ~30–45 min, near a peak ~45, **fading by ~90–120 min**, soft come-down past 3 h with **no redose urge on that route**. Insufflated writeup in the same report calls that route **longer and less rewarding**.
- *The Devil in Crystal Form* (exp104449): multi-day hook / shadow story. Compulsiveness and residue, not a FUN clock. Do not stretch D to cover the binge.
- *DARPA* (exp112117): sleep-dep wreck. After-effects and psychosis, not Peak.

Forum “short bang” = PW **insufflated peak 20–45 min** or **smoked peak 3–6 min**. Vault “main high ~3 h” = oral-shaped Peak. **Locked 90** sits between those: longer than the nasal/vape snap, inside PW oral peak 60–150, under the 3-hour main-high high end, nowhere near after-effects 4–12 h.

### α-PHP — [exp_alphaPHP](https://www.erowid.org/experiences/subs/exp_alphaPHP.shtml)

- *A Dull Double Edged Sword* (exp112036): vape **instant** lightness / body buzz; writer left to talk for **maybe fifteen minutes** and the high was **already disappearing**. Redose to get the peak back. FUN here is a short peak, not the PW smoked total 2–5 h.
- *Surprisingly Functional Stimulation* (exp107041): oral/sublingual — mild stim ~1 h, stronger glow ~2 h, **muted with a redose itch ~2.75 h**, residual **functional** stim later in the night. Writer’s own verdict: duration is **short**, redose pressure is the product. Residual stim ≠ FUN.
- *Perfect Stimulant* / *Have Been Using This Chem for a While*: first-hit poem, then the bag-gone-by-morning compulsiveness already on the house α-PHP card. Clock is vape-short or oral-mid, crash often called kinder than PVP (C/A).

### MDPHP — two reports only

- *A Typical Cathinone High* (exp110228): energy and motivation for a bike ride plus **the first hour of a film** (~90 min of useful lift), then lethargy. “A bit of a lengthy residual stimulation lingering.” A later oral add-on: still awake and working **~3 hours** later, **no comedown felt** — that second clock is leftover / functional, not a peak smile (“no euphoria or pleasant rush”).
- *Not Even Close* (exp107763): title is the review. Stimulation without the rush people wanted from the MDPV/PHP shelf. Too thin to invent a long FUN window.

Do not promote Talk:MDPHP’s 6–12 h oral Total into D. Two vaults plus an unapproved talk page are not a second Ivory Wave clock.

## Map to gameplay D

**D = Peak / still-fun plateau. Not Total. Not Offset. Not After-effects.**

α-PVP is the ruler. Locked **90** already refuses the smoked 3–6 min snap **and** the 4–12 h after-glow. MDPV has to be placed the same way: take the **longer peak**, throw away the 2–48 h residue.

| SKU | Table FUN (Peak / Plateau) | Table leftover (do not ship as D) | Gameplay D |
|---|---|---|---|
| **α-PVP** | PW oral peak **1–2.5 h**; insufflated peak **20–45 min**; smoked peak **3–6 min**. Vault main high ~3 h. I-TREND psych plateau 30–120 min. | PW after 1–12 h by route. I-TREND come-down 5–10 h + next-day. | **90 locked.** Oral-peak / main-high band. Not the vape snap. Not residual wake. |
| **MDPV** | PW oral peak **1–4 h**. Erowid oral plateau **30–180 min**, insufflated plateau **30–120 min**. Vault first-hit smile often **~2 h hold** / ~1.75 h roll-like plateau. PW: “unusually long duration.” | Oral total 2–7 h. After-effects **2–48 h**. Redose-stacked nights. | **180 recommended.** |
| **α-PHP** | PW oral peak **1–2.5 h** (same print as PVP oral). Smoked peak **30–90 min** (much longer *hit* than PVP smoke). Vault: vape smile minutes; oral glow ~2–3 h then leftover function. | Offset 1–5 h oral; after 1–48 h. | Not locked this card. If a later board needs a number: **track oral peak, same rule as PVP → about 90–120.** Do not stamp smoked-total 2–5 h. Do not stamp after-effects. A 55 only makes sense as a *vape-peak* clock; house already refused that move for PVP. |
| **MDPHP** | Unapproved PW oral peak **1–4 h** (same print as MDPV). Smoked peak **1–2 h**. Vault useful lift ~90 min + residual. | Unapproved oral total **6–12 h**; crash 1–4 days. | **Thin.** Do not invent a confident 3-digit D from two vaults + a talk page. If forced: sit **at or under MDPV 180**, never on the 6–12 h Total. A 62 reads like someone copied a short leftover, not the peak. |

### Why MDPV D = 180

1. **Same mapping as the locked 90.** PVP oral peak is 60–150 min; house picked **90** (inside that band, above the nasal snap). MDPV oral peak is **60–240 min**. Mid of that band is 150; Erowid oral plateau **tops at 180**. 180 is the plateau high / peak mid — the same kind of pick as 90, not a Total.
2. **Substantially > 90.** 180 is **2×** the locked PVP FUN window. Matches the house fact: MDPV lasts **way longer** than α-PVP. 100–120 is a bump, not “way longer.”
3. **Not residual.** 180 is under PW oral Total high (7 h) and nowhere near after-effects 2–48 h. Those hours are HalfLife / A.
4. **Vault agrees on shape.** First-hit FUN is a **multi-hour plateau**, then a long wired leftover. Redose adds leftover, not smile. Scoring the leftover as D is how a 48 happens: someone heard “first hit might only last an hour,” halved it, and ignored the 1–4 h peak row.
5. **Erowid’s “duration similar to MDPV” blurb does not veto this.** PW peak rows are not the same (PVP oral 1–2.5 h vs MDPV 1–4 h). House already chose PVP’s FUN shorter than MDPV’s. Keep that.

**Acceptable band if a later card fights 180:** 150–210. All of those are still Peak-shaped and still > 90.

**Fail if shipped**

- MDPV D ≤ 90 (including 48)
- MDPV D copied from after-effects (hundreds of minutes of wake)
- MDPV D = oral Total high (7 h / 420) — that includes offset
- α-PVP D moved off 90
- Any SKU where D = HalfLife
- MDPHP D taken from the unapproved 6–12 h Total

**Recommended catalogue line**

```
α-PVP  Duration = 90     # locked
MDPV   Duration = 180    # FUN window = Erowid oral plateau high / PW oral peak mid
α-PHP  Duration = unset here; if needed 90–120 (oral-peak rule), not vape-total
MDPHP  Duration = unset / thin; if forced ≤ MDPV 180 and not Total
```

HalfLife stays a separate `gameplay_estimate`. This file does not pick those numbers.
