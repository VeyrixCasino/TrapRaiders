# _dur_08 — Route vs perceived duration (MDPV vs α-PVP)

Game catalog. **Not a cook book.** NPC flags only.

No doses. No administration SOP. No injection instructions. This page does **not** rank doors as a menu. It only records which door people *talk about* as a short bang vs a long ride, so later cards can keep `vape_fav` / `oral_ok` / `redose` honest.

**Duration ≠ HalfLife.** Duration is the pleasurable fun window (`gameplay_estimate`). Residual wake, heart-still-going, next-day crash belong on aftereffects / HalfLife. Do not steal those hours into D.

**Catalogue Duration is one number per SKU.** It is the **common recreational fun window**, not the shortest door. A pipe story can be loud and still be the wrong integer.

Do not edit `GAMEPLAY_STATS_*` from this note.

## α-PVP — locked D = 90 is the session, not the pipe

**Route myth:** none locked as “only way it works.” Polish-forum default was nasal. Later English-forum love is the pipe. Fast door exists and is **not** the α-PPP poem.

### What people say the clock did

| Door (NPC talk) | Perceived fun | Residual (not D) |
|---|---|---|
| **Vape / smoked** | Short bang. Wiki-class total in the **half-hour to hour** band. Forum main-euphoria often **twenty to forty-five minutes**, then they reach again. Erowid “Cold Crystal Fire”: vapor was *much shorter* than nasal or oral, even when other posts claimed all doors lasted the same. | Afterglow / wake still hours. That is HalfLife. |
| **Nasal** | Common recreational window. PsychonautWiki total **2–5 h**; naturalistic writeups ~**3 h** of effects, redose itch in the **30–120 min** band. I-TREND Poland: psychological plateau **30–120 min**, then another. Main euphoria in one careful Reddit note ~**2 h**. | Heart and insomnia outlast the grin. |
| **Oral** | Long ride. Wiki-class total **2–6 h**. Clinical recap: up to ~**6 h**. Slower door, same molecule. | After-effects **4–12 h** on the wiki sheet — **not** Duration. |

Forum “shorter bang” is the **pipe**. Catalogue **90** is the **nasal/session smile** — the I-TREND 30–120 min psychological window, not the 3–6 min vapor peak and not the all-night wake. `GAMEPLAY_STATS_B` already said this: residual wake is HalfLife 140, not a reason to move 90.

Do **not** drop α-PVP D to the vape half-hour because NPCs talk pipe. That is what `vape_fav` is for.

## MDPV — common window is hours; the pipe is the lie that made 48

**Route myth:** everything worked and that was the problem. Legal-high years were **oral and nasal** (EMCDDA: those are the common doors; desired effects **2–7 h** on those doors). Foil talk is loud. Finland/Poland even seized blotters. Fast door exists in chemsex / old-head lore — **flag, not a SOP**. Not `slam_only`.

### What people say the clock did

| Door (NPC talk) | Perceived fun | Residual (not D) |
|---|---|---|
| **Oral** | Long ride. Erowid / TripSit / PsychonautWiki / EMCDDA: total **2–7 h**. Plateau up to a few hours. This is the bath-salt / Ivory Wave *session*, not a bang. | After-effects **2–48 h** on the Erowid sheet. Days of wake. **HalfLife / aftereffects.** |
| **Nasal** | Still hours. Erowid / TripSit / EMCDDA: total **2–3.5 h**. Shorter than oral. Still a session, not a pipe. | After-effects table goes ugly (days). Still not D. |
| **Vape / smoked** | Short bang *relative to itself*. Old Bluelight “supercrack”: rushy feel ~**30 min**, then it **settles into a normal MDPV high** instead of falling off a cliff. Vapes **uncleanly** vs α-PVP (can blacken). Loud foil myth. **Not** the legal-high default clock. | Same crash family. Do not fold it into D. |

Bluelight old-head head-to-head (same writer, both bags): MDPV = slower / medium onset, **greater duration**. α-PVP = faster on every door they tried, **lesser duration**. Reddit first-person: α-PVP lasts a shorter amount of time, less residual stim. Molecule clock and door clock both point the same way.

A catalogue **D ≤ 90** for MDPV (including the retracted **48**) is the pipe/rush integer. **Wrong. Do not ship.** Common recreational fun window is the **oral/nasal legal-high session — hours, substantially > 90.** Other `_dur_*` notes own the exact integer. This page only forbids stealing the vape half-hour.

## Same door, still not the same clock

Route changes the *perceived* length. It does not flip the cousins.

| If the NPC used… | α-PVP reads as | MDPV reads as |
|---|---|---|
| Pipe | Shortest bang on this page. Half-hour-class smile. | Short *rush*, then a longer MDPV plateau underneath. Still not as short-only as PVP vapor. |
| Nose | Session. The 90. | Session that still runs **longer** than PVP’s. Hours, not 90. |
| Mouth | Long ride (2–6 h class). | Longer ride (2–7 h class). The one EMCDDA called the common desired-effect window. |

Do not average doors into a fake mid. Do not pick the shortest door because it is cinematic. `vape_fav` is how the NPC brags about the bang. **D is how long the common night was fun.**

## Gaps

- PsychonautWiki has **no** MDPV insufflated / vapor duration table. Nasal hours come from Erowid / TripSit / EMCDDA, not PW.
- Almost no clean single-dose clocks. Redose confounds every forum number (Erowid said this out loud).
- MDPV vapor duration is anecdote-thin; treat `vape_fav` as culture, not a measured half-hour.
- This page does **not** emit MDPV’s integer. It only locks the band: **common fun window = hours, substantially > α-PVP 90**, and forbids the shortest-door steal.
