# Assignment 02 — Extended-chain and N-substituted cathinones

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders Kit E (chain + N-ethyl wall). **Content bible, not a cookbook.**

- **No recipes. No precursor names. No yields. No doses. No injection SOP.**
- Route notes are flags only (`insufflation_common`, `oral_ok`, `vape_talk`, `foil_talk`, `slam_flag`, `mislabel`, `redose`).
- **No opioids.** No pyrovalerones (Kit H–J). No MMC/CMC ring-position tutorial wall (Assignment 01). No bk-MDMA / methylenedioxy cathinones (Kit F).
- Molecular identity is **not** a vendor stamp. A bag saying `4-MPD` or `3-MMC` is a lie until a lab says otherwise.
- **Do not invent names.** Every SKU below is cited. Isomer-only footnotes sit in the coverage report, not on the shelf.

Scores are **forum-inference + official reports**, 0–100 or `null`. Pleasure and compulsiveness are scored **independently**. “Hated it but finished the bag” is high compulsiveness, not low euphoria.

Companion machine file: [`02_cathinones_chain.json`](02_cathinones_chain.json).

## How to read a card

Writeups always go **RUSH → EUPHORIA → COMPULSIVENESS → ENJOYMENT**, then **NEGATIVES**.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Score | 0–100 subjective intensity, or `null` if nobody left a usable feel report |
| Range | Spread across reports, not a dose window |
| Confidence | **A–U**. A = human observational study + large forum + official market. U = name-only isomer footnote |
| `status` | `current_flood` / `market_confirmed` / `historical_wave` / `flop` / `forensic_thin` / `vendor_alias_trap` |

Confidence bands used here:

- **A–C** — human observational or WHO/EUDA review **plus** a fat forum record
- **D–G** — multiple independent forums + seizures
- **H–K** — confirmed market, thin feel
- **L–O** — forensic / checking only, one or two anecdotes
- **P–U** — official name, almost no lived report

## Identity traps (keep these separate)

| Vendor / street stamp | What the lab often found | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **4-MPD** (claimed 4-methylpentedrone) | **4-MEAP** (4-methyl-N-ethylpentedrone) in a 2013–14 vendor sample; vendors also posted the **4-methylbuphedrone** structure and dead CAS numbers | Hamby et al., *J Forensic Sci* 2015 |
| **3-MMC** that lasts till noon | **NEP** (N-ethylnorpentedrone). Cold, long, no hug | EUDA 2025 NEP initial report; in-world Kit E line |
| **NRG-1 / NRG-2 / NRG-3** | Branded legal-high packets. Often **mixed cathinones**, frequently **not** the named molecule (naphyrone bags that were MDPV / mephedrone / pentedrone blends) | Brandt / EMCDDA-era surveys |
| **Hexen / Hex-En / N-ethyl-hex** | Same molecule: **N-ethylhexedrone** (NEH). Not hexedrone | EMCDDA 2015 first seizure; DEA 2019 |
| **NEP / NEPD / ethyl-pentedrone / α-EAPP** | Same molecule: **2-(ethylamino)-1-phenylpentan-1-one**. EUDA prefers **N-ethylnorpentedrone** | EUDA 2025 |
| **HEP** | Street tag on **N-ethylheptedrone**, also sold as NEP / MDMA / meth / benzos | WHO 47th ECDD 2024 |
| **4-MMC-MeO** | Vendor name for **mexedrone** (α-methoxy mephedrone). Not mephedrone | Roberts et al. 2017; Bluelight flop thread |
| **Bath salts / plant food / research chemical / face powder** | Packet theater. Not a substance | Hamby 2015; DEA 2019 |

**Isomer pile-up (same formula, different SKU):** NEP shares a formula with hexedrone, 4-MPD, 4-EEC, 4-methylbuphedrone, 3,4-DMEC, NEB, 4-EMC, 3-MEC, diethylcathinone, isohexedrone, 2-MEB, and more. GC-MS alone lies. This is why the wall needs **separate stickers**, not “pentedrone family dust.”

## Cards

### NEP — N-ethylnorpentedrone

**One-liner:** If it lasts till noon, it wasn’t 3-MMC.

**IDs:** 2-(ethylamino)-1-phenylpentan-1-one. Aliases (not extra molecules): NEP, NEPD, N-ethylpentedrone, ethyl-pentedrone, N-ethylnorpentedrone, α-EAPP. Family cathinone / subclass pentedrone_chain. Status: **current_flood**.

Austria notified EMCDDA 6 Mar 2014 off a Styria powder from Oct 2013. Uchiyama had already bought it on the open net Jan–Aug 2013. EUDA intensive monitoring Oct 2024. ~11 tonnes of powder in the Union; 99% of that since 2022; Netherlands imports from India did the heavy lifting. 25 Member States. Sixty-three deaths with confirmed exposure (six states). Typical form: white / off-white / yellow powder. Tablets, liquids, herb — rare.

**Rush.** Fast off the nose. Núñez-Montero 2025 (n=8, checked powder, club setting): subjective effects inside 20 min, gone 3–4 h. Faster on, faster off than hexen. Forum split: “almost no rush, just a slide” vs “too rushy and sweaty when the bump was greedy.” Call the rush **mid, not hexen’s punch**. Score **58** (range 40–75, conf **B**).

**Euphoria.** Observational VAS “liking” peaked higher than hexen (+72 vs +39 mm). Erowid: coke + a little MDMA body, “clean” when the bump stays polite. Bluelight: nice hour-and-a-half glow, then the Goldilocks zone or psychosis-wiggle. Not a hug like 4-MMC. Score **68** (50–80, **B**).

**Compulsiveness.** This is the number that matters. People who **do not like it** still stay up 36 hours. “Very compulsive for a chemical that doesn’t provide much euphoria.” Vape talk makes it worse. EUDA + fora: frequent redose / binge. Sold as 3-MMC, so the bag outlives the night. Score **86** (70–95, **B**). High compulsiveness, **not** a verdict on pleasure.

**Enjoyment.** Split personality. Some: “brilliant coke alternative, no nasty cluck.” Some: “anxiety first, then I kept going anyway.” Observational “bad effects” were real (+40 mm). Score **62** (35–80, **C**).

**Sociability.** Observational: “open,” “close to others,” “want to be with people” were the **top** VAS items. Erowid: conversations that would not have happened. Forum: also porn-and-podcast hermit mode. Score **70** (40–85, **C**).

**Negatives.** Paranoia / psychotic wiggle after the session overstays (**72**, **C**). Aftereffects: some swear “no comedown,” binge reports say 36-hour wreckage (**60**, **C**). Cardiovascular: SBP +21, HR +22 in the observational; palpitations in Erowid (**78**, **B**). Sleep: the all-nighter is the product (**85**, **B**).

**Route myth:** `insufflation_common` `redose` `mislabel` `vape_talk` `oral_ok`.

### Hexen — N-ethylhexedrone (NEH)

**One-liner:** Coke’s ugly twin who still steals the night.

**IDs:** 2-(ethylamino)-1-phenylhexan-1-one. Aliases: hexen, Hex-En, NEH, N-ethyl-hex, ethyl-hexedrone. **Not** hexedrone. First EMCDDA hit 2015. Boehringer patent dust from 1964, street life from 2015. DEA temp Schedule I with 4-MEAP in 2019. UK 2019–23: 139 detections (8th most seen cathinone in that ACMD table). UNODC: among the most seized SCs worldwide.

**Rush.** This is the one people compare to coke. Bluelight: “the rush, the compulsive redosing, the comedown — it just straight reminded me of coke.” Slam talk exists; **flag only**. Observational: onset inside 20 min, a hair slower and duller at the peak than NEP. Forum rush sits **above** NEP. Score **78** (60–90, **B**).

**Euphoria.** “Perfect euphoric stimulant — if you have perfect discipline.” First bump can be amazing; redoses go cold and jittery. Observational liking was **lower** than NEP. Erowid title that stuck: “Best Dope, Worst Comedown.” Score **72** (50–85, **B**).

**Compulsiveness.** The rat-in-a-box chemical. “I really know I don’t need it and it would be pointless to do a bump right now but you still fucking do it.” Stronger fiend than NEP in the old head-to-head. Score **92** (80–98, **B**).

**Enjoyment.** High on bump one. Collapses if the night continues. Score **64** (25–85, **C**). “Loved it / crushed me” is not a contradiction.

**Sociability.** Functional, talky, then messy. Observational empathy scores were real but softer than NEP. Score **58** (35–75, **D**).

**Negatives.** Paranoia / psychosis after a binge (**80**, **C**). Aftereffects: the famous brutal comedown (**88**, **B**). Cardiovascular: SBP +22, HR +21; fatal and non-fatal case pile (**82**, **B**). Sleep: gone (**88**, **B**). Nose damage is folklore, not a recipe.

**Route myth:** `insufflation_common` `redose` `vape_talk` `slam_flag`.

### 4-MEC — 4-methylethcathinone

**One-liner:** You can go to work. You will hate work.

**IDs:** 2-(ethylamino)-1-(4-methylphenyl)propan-1-one. EMCDDA first: UK, 8 Jul 2010. WHO ECDD 2014 + 2016. Branded legal-high constituent, not just a research-chem bag. Paris syringe residue study 2012: 23% of 3,489 syringes held 4-MEC (EUDA POD — **slam_flag**). Barcelona harm-reduction: 4-MEC was the NPS people named.

**Rush.** Erowid “Four Cathinones” study: an **intense, almost panic** onset that then leaves almost nothing. Redoses “barely any effect.” Other reports: short, burn-y, coke-adjacent kick. Score **60** (35–80, **C**).

**Euphoria.** WHO user-report analysis: euphoria, wellbeing, stim — **less intense and shorter than mephedrone**. Some call it “rolly”; some call it junk. Score **58** (30–75, **C**).

**Compulsiveness.** WHO: **conflicting** on urge / craving / binge. Erowid 99725: low inhibition, kept lining for hours. The Four Cathinones author: boosters did nothing. Score **64** (30–85, **D**) — the conflict is real; do not flatten it.

**Enjoyment.** Diet meow. Fun, short, burn. “Won’t buy again” sits next to “I liked this drug.” Score **52** (25–70, **C**).

**Sociability.** Mild. Not 4-MMC talky. Score **48** (25–65, **D**).

**Negatives.** Sweating, nausea, jaw, nystagmus, palpitations, migraine, “loss of sight” in the WHO list. Paranoia **45** (**D**). Aftereffects: some claim no comedown, some get wrecked at higher oral totals (**50**, **C**). Cardiovascular **68** (**C**). Sleep **55** (**D**) — short duration saves some nights.

**Route myth:** `insufflation_common` `oral_ok` `redose` `slam_flag` `branded_legal_high`.
