# Assignment 17 — Diarylethylamine / other dissociative classes

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a manual.** No recipes. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. Scores are unitless **0–100 or null** inferences from public reports. **Rush / euphoria / enjoyment / compulsiveness are scored independently** — a hated night can still empty the bag.

Four **separate** families share this file because they are all non-opioid dissociatives that are **not** ketamine / MXE / PCP. They are not analogues of each other. Do not stamp DXM as a diphenidine cousin. Do not put nitrous on the Flask wall.

---

## Scope

**In (Kit W headliners):** diphenidine, ephenidine, MXP / methoxphenidine / 2-MXP, fluorolintane.

**In (documented diaryl relatives):** isophenidine / NPDPA; lefetamine (historical parent, more stim); 2-chlorodiphenidine (paper analog); 2-chloro-ephenidine (tiny 2014–15 vendor moment); 2-MeO-ephenidine (name exists, identity shaky).

**In (isomer stamps, not extra bottles):** 3-MXP, 4-MXP. Sold product was **2-MXP**. McLaughlin 2016 exists to tell the lab which isomer is in the bag.

**In (other families, keep separate):**
- Morphinan: **DXM**, **DXO** (metabolite lore, almost never a bag)
- Inhaled gas: **nitrous oxide**
- Aminoadamantane: **memantine**, **amantadine** (thin)
- Historical inhalant anesthetic: **diethyl ether** (museum crate)

**Out (owned elsewhere):** ketamine, DCK, 2F-DCK, 2F-NENDCK, FXE, HXE, NENK, 2-MeO-ketamine, 2-BDCK, tiletamine. MXE, DMXE, MXPr, MXiPr, O-PCE, MXM. PCP, 3-MeO-PCP, 3-HO-PCP, 3-Me-PCP, 3-Cl-PCP, 3-F-PCP, 4-MeO-PCP, PCE, 3-MeO-PCE, 3-HO-PCE, PCPy, PCMo, TCP.

**Out (hard ban / wrong class):** MT-45, AD-1211, diphenpipenol (diarylethylamine *opioids*). Salvia / salvinorin (kappa-opioid). DPH / diphenhydramine (deliriant, assignment 29 — and the “DPH” nickname for diphenidine is a trap). GHB/GBL (sedative). Glue / toluene / chloroform (not a catalog family). MK-801 / dizocilpine, lanicemine, remacemide (bench / pharma, no Port Rook bag).

## Which diaryls people actually remember

Street memory is a three-name joke plus one dusty bottle.

1. **Ephenidine** — the diaryl people remember *as a night*. Cosmic, forced, long residual. House line: **not a hole, a kidnapping.** Some later threads insist you *can* hole; the thing they write about is still the abduction, not ket’s clean drop. Afterglow measured in days, not songs.
2. **MXP** — remembered as a **sticker**, not a favorite. Sold as the MXE replacement the week MXE died. Mostly disappointed. Roller-coaster, slow, confusing, some afterglow fans. House line: **they printed MXE on the hope.**
3. **Diphenidine** — remembered as the **lie**. Steep, dumb, long. Japan herbal-incense deaths (mixed with synthetic cannabinoids). House line: **legal ket that lies.**
4. **Fluorolintane** — name on a list. ACMD: no WEDINOS hits, no Euro-DEN, no deaths. Reddit: “available for a week a few years ago.” House line: **one bottle.**

Everyone else on the diaryl sheet is chemist / forensic memory: isophenidine, lefetamine, 2-Cl-DPH, 3-/4-MXP, 2-Cl-ephenidine. Do not put them on the v1 wall.

**Non-diaryl names this file owns that NPCs actually say:** **DXM** (corner-store disso) and **nitrous** (balloons). Memantine is a niche “Namenda week.” DXO is trivia.

## How the diaryl shelf feels (one board)

Diaryls are **not** a close analogue of MXE even when the bag says MXE. Slower onset than ket. Longer night. Steeper stairs. Motor wreck. The hole, if it arrives, is less of a room and more of a kidnapping or a dumb blackout.

| Slider | High | Mid | Low / empty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enjoyment** | **Ephenidine** | 2-Cl-ephenidine (one voice) | MXP (polarizing flop), diphenidine, fluorolintane |
| **Euphoria** | Ephenidine | MXP afterglow crowd | Diphenidine (clears then sours) |
| **Rush** | Vapor rumor (all of them) | Ephenidine come-up buzz | Oral MXP / diphenidine (slow stairs) |
| **Compulsiveness** | Diphenidine (chase the steep), MXP session | Ephenidine (acute tolerance kills the encore) | Fluorolintane (nobody had a bag long enough) |
| **Dissociation depth** | Ephenidine forced / diphenidine dumb-deep | MXP “hole from hell” reports | Fluorolintane unknown |
| **Confusion** | **Diphenidine**, MXP | Ephenidine (clearer than the piperidines) | — |
| **Warmth** | Ephenidine | MXP afterglow | Diphenidine (sharp / dumb) |

Nervewing-style ranking does not exist for this shelf the way it does for ACH. The stable language is: **ephenidine = the one with magic; MXP = the MXE hope; diphenidine = the steep liar.**

## Agent cards

Scores: 0–100 or `unknown`. Pleasure ≠ compulsiveness. Confidence A/B/C/D/U.

### Diphenidine (DPD, 1,2-DEP, DND; sometimes wrongly “DPH”)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: Legal ket that lies.

Piperidine diaryl. Showed up right after the 2013 UK arylcyclohexylamine ban. Japanese “herbal incense” bags mixed it with synthetic cannabinoids; one of those mixes is a death headline. STRIDA (Sweden 2014) actually caught it in blood. WEDINOS saw a handful of powders 2013–16, then it vanished. ACMD: stimulant + neuropsychiatric tox (agitation, confusion, tachycardia), 48 worldwide deaths in the diphenidine/MXP pile, most of them MXP, a few this.

PW: low-end “euphoric and clear-headed”; moderate-plus can **turn confusing and dysphoric with no warning**. Bizarre somatosensory stuff, transient amnesia, motor wreck. Geometry dark and bland vs ket. Compulsive redose listed. Not a party favor. The steepness is the rumor — people chase a hole and get a kidnapping that is dumber than ephenidine’s.

#### The hit

- **Rush 42 (C):** Not a ket slam by mouth. Slow stairs. Vapor talk exists (`vape_fav` myth); do not write a how-to. The “rush” people wanted was ket’s and they did not get it.
- **Euphoria 48 (B):** Real at the low end (tingle in hands/feet/head, music). Then it can sour for no reason. That souring is the brand.
- **Enjoyment 38 (B):** The remembered night is a bad one. People bought it as legal ket. It lied. A few like the stim-disso walk. Most do not write hymns.
- **Compulsiveness 55 (B):** Steep curve plus “maybe the next one holes.” Session chase. Not α-PVP, not O-PCE. Still empties a night.

#### The bill

Confusion, motor loss, amnesia, nausea at the peak, mania/psychosis rumor (common to stim-dissos), tachycardia. Japan spice-mix deaths are **poly-bag** (`mislabel`), not a clean solo tox story. Residual can last into the next day. `freakout` is the house flag.

NPC line: “Legal ket that lies.”  
Flags: `freakout` `mislabel` `vape_fav`  
Gaps: Solo vs spice-mix harm is muddy. “DPH” alias collides with diphenhydramine — never print that on a sign.

### Ephenidine (NEDPA, EPE, N-ethyl-1,2-diphenylethylamine)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: Not a hole. A kidnapping.

Open-chain N-ethyl diaryl. Same 2013–16 wave. Marketed next to diphenidine/MXP as an MXE stand-in; users immediately said **the effects were not MXE**. WEDINOS actually saw it (2015–17). ACMD found **no** published acute-tox case series and **no** deaths with this name on the certificate — that is absence of paperwork, not a safety medal. Kang et al. 2016: ketamine-like NMDA block plus weaker DAT/NET. French Addictovigilance: a few ephenidine mentions inside a mostly-MXP pile.

This is the diaryl trip reports call **cosmic / forced**. Soft all-over body high. Clearer head than DXM or ket at the walk-around end. Visuals more psychedelic (drifting) than diphenidine/MXP. Music. Time dilation. Residual that outlasts the playlist — “afterglow” talk in days-to-weeks, not hours. House contract is correct: people argue about whether a *hole* exists; what they describe is being **taken**. A 2016 reddit thread (“you can hole and it is amazing”) still reads like a theme-park abduction you can briefly surface from, not ket’s short room. Bluelight: “finally a worthy alternative” from people who had already burned out on MXE/ket — and also ER stories that took days to leave.

#### The hit

- **Rush 40 (C):** A little stimulation on the come-up (Bluelight first reports). Not a slam. The night arrives; it does not punch.
- **Euphoria 78 (B):** The high score on this shelf. PW: particularly euphoric vs DXM/ket. Roi on Bluelight: euphoric, introspective, “a certain magic.” That is why this name survived.
- **Enjoyment 82 (B):** The diaryl people remember. Cosmic without MXE’s legend tax. Forced, long, worth the stamp for the ones who liked being kidnapped.
- **Compulsiveness 58 (B):** Compulsive redose is on the PW list. Acute tolerance (same-night encore dies) caps the binge. People still sit in it too long because the residual feels like the drug never clocked out.

#### The bill

Motor wreck, robotic gait, amnesia, nausea (inconsistent), long residual that can feel like the kidnapping did not end. `freakout` when the forced part wins. Not a bladder story in the ACMD pile (no evidence of ket-style urotoxicity for this class — still do not advertise “safe”).

NPC line: “Not a hole. A kidnapping.”  
Flags: `freakout`  
Gaps: Hole-vs-kidnap split is forum weather. Paper trail is thinner than MXP’s death stack; do not read that as “safer.”

### MXP — methoxphenidine / methoxyphenidine / 2-MXP / 2-MeO-diphenidine

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: They printed MXE on the hope.

2-methoxy diphenidine. **This** is the isomer that shipped. Vendors said MXE replacement the week MXE got heat. Van Hout & Hearne 2015 (“Word of mouse”): steep rise in interest, vendor caution that feedback was thin, marketed as legal MXE / diphenidine / ket, dissociative-plus-stim wave that could last for **days**, hypertension and seizure talk, memory-loss and limb-numbness rumor, afterglow “empowerment.” PW: body high closer to **DXM** than to ket; “clear-headed” even heavy; controllable disconnection; afterglow some people liked as much as the night. Forum majority: **not as fun as MXE**, slow oral, spinning / roller-coaster. “Hole from hell” reports exist — delirious, long, not a room you wanted. ACMD/WHO: this name owns most of the diaryl death certificates (UK 2014–17 cluster). IONA and Euro-DEN actually caught it.

#### The hit

- **Rush 35 (B):** Slow stairs. Not insufflatable “like MXE” in the early reddit. Spinning is not a rush. Vapor rumor (`vape_fav`) is faster; still not the MXE punch they paid for.
- **Euphoria 52 (B):** Soft-body vibration, then it can go blank. Afterglow euphoria is the part fans defend. Peak vs MXE is the disappointment.
- **Enjoyment 44 (B):** A flop as a replacement and a real chemical. Some people liked the control and the afterglow. Most people who wanted Mexxy got a long confusing cousin and wrote the review that killed the sticker.
- **Compulsiveness 50 (B):** Session redose, “maybe it will turn into MXE.” Van Hout duration-for-days is a trap, not a hug. One published withdrawal-ish hospital case after a few weeks (Champeau 2017) — treat as a single story, not a law.

#### The bill

Confusion, motor loss, lagged audio, hypertension / tachycardia, seizure rumor, memory holes, limb numbness rumor, long residual. Deaths are real and mostly this name, often poly-drug. `mislabel` is the origin story (MXE on the hope). Not a bladder class in the ACMD note — still an NMDA disso.

NPC line: “They printed MXE on the hope.”  
Flags: `mislabel` `vape_fav`  
Gaps: 3-MXP / 4-MXP bags were a lab problem, not a second street drug. Afterglow fans vs “never again” never got a study.

### Fluorolintane (2-F-DPPy, 1-[1-(2-fluorophenyl)-2-phenylethyl]pyrrolidine)

Status: limited_evidence  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: One bottle.

Pyrrolidine diaryl with a 2-fluoro. Colestock / Wallach 2019: NMDA affinity plus **DAT higher than NMDA** on the bench, PPI disruption in rats, “consistent with anecdotal dissociative effects.” ACMD: **no** WEDINOS, **no** Euro-DEN, **no** deaths, **no** TOXBASE card. Reddit 2020: “was it only available for a week a few years ago.” House card is the whole street file.

#### The hit

- **Rush unknown (U)**
- **Euphoria unknown (U)**
- **Enjoyment unknown (U)** — do not invent a vibe from DAT numbers.
- **Compulsiveness unknown (U)** — a tertiary guide claims “more compulsive, shorter than diphenidine.” That is not a report pile. Leave null.

#### The bill

Unknown in humans. Bench says stim-disso is plausible. Positional fluoro isomers and un-fluorinated 1,2-DEPy were studied as chemistry, not as bags — do not SKU them.

NPC line: “One bottle.”  
Flags: —  
Gaps: Human report volume is basically zero. If a crate appears, it is a dusty Easter egg, not a wall.

### DXM — dextromethorphan

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: Corner-store disso. Not Flask.

Morphinan. Cough aisle. Completely different scaffold from diaryls. Sigma + serotonin + NMDA; the “robo” walk, the platters, the cheap cosmic that feels like a radiator. House: dirty, platters, `freakout`. Music, nostalgia, itchy, nauseous, next-day wool. People redose through plateaus because the bottle is still there. This is the disso **kids and broke NPCs** name. Do not put it on the Kit W shelf. Do not compare it to diphenidine except to say **it is not that**.

#### The hit

- **Rush 28 (A):** There is no slam. A long greasy climb. People who want a rush buy something else and come back ashamed.
- **Euphoria 58 (A):** Wonky, warm-in-the-skull, music, cartoon gravity. Real. Also itchy.
- **Enjoyment 52 (A):** Polarizing. Cheap magic for some. “Never again, I can still taste it” for others. Do not average the hate into low euphoria — the night can be fun and still be dirty.
- **Compulsiveness 70 (A):** The bottle is in the house. Plateaus are a ladder. High school / broke-adult lore is a redose culture. Not pyro-fiend, not nothing.

#### The bill

Itch, nausea, next-day fog, robotic walk in public (`freakout`), serotonin-combination scare (do not write a combo list). Coricidin-type multi-symptom bags are a **different, dirtier product** (antihistamine on board) — if the crate is CCC, that is a `mislabel` / bad-SKU flag, not “stronger DXM.”

NPC line: “Corner-store disso. Not Flask.”  
Flags: `freakout` `redose`  
Gaps: HBr vs polistirex is the same molecule, different clock — one SKU, two clocks, no second name.

### Nitrous oxide (N2O, laughing gas, balloons, whippets, chargers)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: Balloons. Thirty seconds. One more charger.

Not a powder. Not NMDA-primary in the diaryl sense — a blink of anesthesia plus hypoxia rumor plus the B12 / nerve-damage pile that is now a systematic review, not a vibe. Club crate. Dentists and kitchens are the joke origin. The **rush is the product**. The compulsiveness is the product. The night is a pile of empty chargers and a song that kept restarting.

#### The hit

- **Rush 92 (A):** The only true rush in this file. Instant dissolve. Then air. People stack because the first one taught their hands the motion.
- **Euphoria 80 (A):** Giggling, cartoon, dissolve, music-as-cathedral for a breath. Real, short, gone.
- **Enjoyment 78 (A):** Balloons are a party noun. The hangover is not the night. Chronic stackers still talk like they had fun until their legs stop.
- **Compulsiveness 90 (A):** “Just one more.” Highest fiend in this assignment, lowest duration. Independent of whether they *liked* the last one.

#### The bill

Empty, cold-burn / frostbite rumor from tanks, B12 functional-deficiency neuropathy (paresthesia, walk-wrong — this is paper, not forum weather), hypoxia if the room is the bag. Not a Flask SKU. Not a wall.

NPC line: “Balloons. Thirty seconds.”  
Flags: `redose`  
Gaps: Tank vs charger vs medical is packaging, not a new molecule.

### Memantine (Namenda)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: street (diversion)  
Headline: Namenda week. Fog with a prescription font.

Aminoadamantane. Weak uncompetitive NMDA. Dementia medicine that a disso niche swallowed because it lasts **a long time** and does not feel like ket. Functional, anxious, motor-clumsy, days of residual. Almost no rush. Some people chase a “kick in the teeth” long fog (Erowid combo reports exist). Not Kit W. Not DXM. Not a balloon.

#### The hit

- **Rush 8 (B):** Glacial. If someone says it rushed them, they stacked something else.
- **Euphoria 35 (B):** Mild lift or none. Often just strange.
- **Enjoyment 48 (C):** Niche. The functional crowd likes being slightly not-themselves for a work week. Others call it empty anxiety.
- **Compulsiveness 28 (B):** Too long to binge like ket. People still redose because they forgot they already took it.

#### The bill

Days of fog, clumsiness, anxiety, nausea in the climb. Combo-with-nitrous reports exist — that is two families stacked, not a new SKU.

NPC line: “Namenda week. You will still be on it on Thursday.”  
Flags: —  
Gaps: Prescription dose vs recreational-scene dose is a how-to; we do not write it. Score the *shape*: long, weak, no rush.

**Alias traps:** DPH → diphenhydramine, not diphenidine. 2-MXP = MXP. 3-MXP/4-MXP ≠ extra headliners.  
**Explicitly not duplicated:** ket / MXE / PCP shelves; diaryl opioids; salvia; DPH.

**Strength:** Three diaryls have a stable *street language* (lie / hope / kidnapping) that matches the house contract. MXP has the paper trail (deaths, Van Hout, WEDINOS). DXM and nitrous are culturally saturated.  
**Weakness:** Ephenidine is loved in trip reports and almost missing from tox registries. Fluorolintane / isophenidine are names. DAT-heavy bench data for fluorolintane is not a feel card. Diaryl “vape/smoke” talk is a flag, not a recipe.

**Diaryls people actually remember:** **ephenidine, MXP, diphenidine.** In that order for *nights*. MXP wins *headlines*. Fluorolintane is a bottle in a drawer.

## Gaps

- No head-to-head human study of ephenidine vs MXP vs diphenidine. Rankings are forum weather plus death certificates.
- Ephenidine hole-vs-kidnap split unresolved; house inference stays kidnapping.
- MXP 3-/4-isomer bags: lab story, not a second scene.
- 2-MeO-ephenidine identity (2 vs 3) never cleaned up.
- Fluorolintane human file is empty; DAT>NMDA is not a vibe.
- Diaryl urotoxicity: ACMD says no evidence. Do not advertise safety.
- DXO-as-product: almost certainly not a Port Rook SKU.
- Memantine recreational vs prescription clock is a how-to; we only scored shape.
- Ether / solvent crate must never become a tutorial.
- Japan incense deaths are poly-bags (diaryl + synthetic cannabinoid). `mislabel`, not a clean LD story.
