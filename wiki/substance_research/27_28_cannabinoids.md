# Assignments 27+28 — Cannabinoids (spice + plant / hemp)

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a cookbook.**

- No recipes, precursors, yields, glassware, or how-to doses.
- Route notes are NPC flags only (`vape_fav`, `smoke`, `edible`, `mislabel`, `spray`).
- No opioids.
- Scores are **0–100 or unknown**. Unknown is `null`, never `0`.
- Lead every card with **rush / euphoria / compulsiveness / enjoyment**. Negatives below. A hated binge can still score high compulsiveness.
- **Spice is compulsive and nasty, not fun.** Early JWH could fake “strong weed.” Later gens are a kidnapping with a lighter. Do not score them as cannabis with extra zeros.
- Do not invent 200 empty analog SKUs. Full cards = reports or a real seizure/EWS pile. The rest are **names-only**.
- Pharma similarity is not a family. Dronabinol is delta-9-THC in a bottle. Nabilone is a medicine analog, not a Spice cousin. Rimonabant is an anti-high.

**27 owns** CB1/CB2 research-chemical “Spice / K2” SCRAs.

**28 owns** phytocannabinoids, hemp semisynthetics, and distinct cannabinoid-related families that are **not** aminoalkylindole / indazole-carboxamide spice.

EUDA EDR 2025: 277 cannabinoids monitored; 20 new in 2024, **18 of them semi-synthetic**. China July 2021 class ban wrecked MDMB-4en-PINACA / ADB-BUTINACA supply; OXIZIDs appeared and **never became widespread**. HHC is the European semisynthetic headliner.

## How to read a card

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Status | `well_documented` / `moderately_documented` / `limited_evidence` / `chemical_literature_only` / `identity_uncertain` / `alias_duplicate` / `unverified_claim` |
| Market | `street` / `rc_era` / `literature_only` / `uncertain` |
| The hit | Rush, euphoria, enjoyment, compulsiveness — independent |
| The bill | Negatives only if evidenced |

Rush on smoked cannabinoids is a **punch**, not cocaine. Oral / edible is a melt — score rush low or `null`. Compulsiveness is the next bowl / the next cone, not “I loved it.”

Confidence: **A** many independent reports + official; **B** several strands; **C** thin; **D** analog inference; **U** no feel.

## 27 — Generation wall (do not flatten)

SCRAs are one **feel family** (CB1 full-agonist punch) and many **stamp generations**. EMCDDA splits molecules as tail / linker / core / linked group. Game kits can reuse one “sprayed herb / foil / vape liquid” mesh. Do not print a theoretical isomer grid.

| Gen | Years (EU/US street) | Core idea | Headliners |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — CP / HU** | 2008 Spice first labs | Cyclohexylphenol / classical THC-like | CP 47,497, CP 47,497-C8, HU-210 |
| **1 — JWH** | 2008–2011 | Naphthoylindole (Huffman) | JWH-018, 073, 081, 122, 210, 250 |
| **1b — AM** | 2010–2013 | Makriyannis fluoro / iodo stamps | AM-2201, AM-694, AM-1220 |
| **2 — UR / XLR** | 2011–2014 | Tetramethylcyclopropyl | UR-144, XLR-11 |
| **2b — esters** | 2013–2015 | Quinolinyl carboxylate | PB-22, 5F-PB-22 |
| **3 — PINACA** | 2012–2017 | Indazole carboxamide, pentyl | AB-PINACA, 5F-ADB, 5F-AMB, AKB48 |
| **3b — FUBINACA** | 2013–2018 | 4-fluorobenzyl tail | AB-FUBINACA, ADB-FUBINACA, AMB-FUBINACA |
| **3c — CHMINACA / CHMICA** | 2014–2017 | Cyclohexylmethyl | AB-CHMINACA, ADB-CHMINACA, MDMB-CHMICA |
| **4 — ADB / MDMB later** | 2016–2021 | Shorter tails, 4-en, PICA | 5F-MDMB-PICA, 4F-MDMB-BINACA, MDMB-4en-PINACA, ADB-BUTINACA |
| **4b — CUMYL / SGT** | 2014–2019 | Cumyl / γ-carbolinone | CUMYL-PEGACLONE, CUMYL-4CN-BINACA |
| **5 — post-2021** | 2021– | OXIZID, IATA, bromo-core | BZO-HEXOXIZID, ADB-FUBIATA, CH-PIATA — **thin feel, seizure names** |

Play the whole spice wall as: **fast smoked punch, short magic, high redose, panic / zombie / heart tax.** Early JWH is the only generation people sometimes remember as “fun.”

## CP 47,497 / CP 47,497-C8 (CP-47,497, C8 homolog)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: **The other powder in the first Spice tin. Not JWH. Still a CB1 hammer.**

### The hit
Rush **48 (B)** — Smoked herb onset, not a stim snap. First-wave “incense” that actually worked.  
Euphoria **44 (C)** — Stoned, heavy, flatter than plant. Thin first-person pile next to JWH-018.  
Enjoyment **40 (C)** — People bought *Spice Gold*, not this name.  
Compulsiveness **62 (C)** — Short enough to re-light. Inferred from class + early legal-high binge talk.

### The bill
Sedation, hangover fog, “not weed.” C8 homolog was the one Auwärter / THC Pharm actually pulled from 2008 German Spice. Negatives thinner than later carboxamides because the dose-on-the-leaf was less of a lottery.

NPC line: *"Before they named the generations, this was already in the tin."*

Sources: Auwärter et al. 2009 *J Mass Spectrom* (Spice); EMCDDA 2009 Spice action; UNODC early SCRA reviews.  
Gaps: Almost nobody wrote a CP-only trip. Scores lean on the 2008 product, not a clean molecule file.

## JWH-018 (AM-678, first-wave Spice)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: **The one that taught the world Spice was not incense.**

### The hit
Rush **64 (A)** — Smoked: a fast CB1 punch. People who wanted stronger weed got a slap. Not cocaine. Oral is a late brick.  
Euphoria **58 (A)** — Real stoned lift for a short window. Music, heavy body, giggles if the leaf was weak. Then the room tilts.  
Enjoyment **54 (B)** — Highest *fun* on this wall. Early heads compared it to strong cannabis. Later heads remember the panic more than the hug. Do not average 2014 horror into 2009 reviews.  
Compulsiveness **78 (A)** — Bowl, cone, cone. Short peak, greedy re-light. “Hated the anxiety, finished the packet.”

### The bill
Anxiety spike, tachycardia, red eyes that look wrong, next-morning flat. Seizures and psychosis show up in the tox pile, especially when the leaf was hotter than the label. Withdrawal / rebound anxiety after daily incense is documented in case series. `smoke` `mislabel`.

NPC line: *"First Spice. Strong weed that files a complaint."*

Sources: Auwärter 2009; EMCDDA 2009–2011 JWH-018 risk notes; Erowid Spice / JWH-018 vaults; Every-Palmer psychosis series; UN 2015 scheduling (JWH-018 + AM-2201).  
Gaps: Brand-on-the-tin is not the molecule. Early “Spice Gold” was mixed.
