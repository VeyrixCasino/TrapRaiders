# Assignment 13 — Ketamine-type dissociatives (Kit K)

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a cookbook.** No recipes. No precursors. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. Scores are unitless **0–100 or null**. **Rush here means onset salience**, not a stim snap. Enjoyment, euphoria, compulsiveness, and reinforcement are **independent** — a hated redose loop is not low enjoyment (ketamine), and a loved couch is not a fiend (HXE).

Family: 2-oxo arylcyclohexylamines that play as **ketamine-likes** — ortho-halogen / ortho-MeO / 2-thienyl, short-to-mid hole fantasy. FXE and HXE sit chemically on the 3-sub 2-oxo-PCE side; Kit K still owns the street stickers. **MXE / O-PCE / PCP / PCE families are other assignments.** Do not steal those SKUs.

---

## Who people actually chase

This kit is not a wall of equals. Forum and drug-checking traffic collapse to **five hunts**:

| Rank | SKU | Why they chase it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ketamine** | The hole people mean. Kitchen-door. Party / clinic. Always. |
| 2 | **DCK** | The analog old heads actually *miss*. Longer, cleaner, still a hole if you insist. |
| 3 | **2F-DCK** | Volume chase when ket is dry. Closest clone — **batch lottery**. |
| 4 | **2F-NENDCK / CanKet** | Late Flask after 2F dried. Also the powder inside most bags stamped FXE or ket. |
| 5 | **FXE (the name)** | Sold as the MXE-shaped ket. Labs keep rewriting the sticker to CanKet. |

HXE is liked, not hunted — couch bottle. NENK, 2-MeO-ketamine, 2-BDCK, tiletamine are leftovers or jokes. Thin names (2-TFMDCK, NPNK, 2-FXPr, 2-MeO-NEK, tilmetamine) are watch-list dust.

Street rack (this kit only):

```
[ { ketamine, DCK, 2F-DCK, 2F-NENDCK, FXE, HXE }
  + dusty { NENK, 2-MeO-ketamine, 2-BDCK, tiletamine } ]
```

## Scope

**In (full cards):** ketamine, DCK, 2F-DCK, NENK, 2F-NENDCK, FXE, HXE, 2-MeO-ketamine, 2-BDCK, tiletamine.

**In (thin / mostly null):** 2-TFMDCK, NPNK (propylketamine), 2-FXPr (2F-2oxo-PCPr), 2-MeO-NEK, tilmetamine.

**Alias, not a second agent:** see merge table. **3-FXE ≠ 2-FXE.** Street “FXE” after ~2022 is usually CanKet.

**Out (owned elsewhere):** MXE, DMXE, MXPr, MXiPr, MXM. O-PCE / 2-oxo-PCE / deschloro-N-ethyl-ketamine. PCP and 3-sub PCP. PCE, 3-MeO-PCE, 3-HO-PCE, 3-Me-PCE, PCPr / PCMo / TCP. Diarylethylamines. DXM. Nitrous.

**Not a SKU:** esketamine / arketamine / Spravato (ketamine’s hands). Norketamine and hydroxynorketamines (metabolites). Telazol / Zoletil (tiletamine **+ zolazepam**). Tusi / pink cocaine (color, usually ket ± extras).

## How this shelf feels (one board)

For dissos, **The hit** still leads. Map: Rush ≈ how fast/loud the room changes. Euphoria = the hug. Enjoyment = would they buy it again. Compulsiveness = the bump-loop or the weekend-loop. Then one line on **hole vs walk**. Negatives sit below: bladder rumor, confusion, residual.

| Slider | High | Mid | Low / empty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enjoyment** | ketamine, DCK | 2F-NENDCK, FXE (if genuine), 2F-DCK (good shards) | NENK, 2-BDCK, 2-MeO-ket, tiletamine |
| **Euphoria** | ketamine, DCK | FXE, HXE, 2F-NENDCK | NENK, 2-MeO-ket, 2-BDCK |
| **Rush (onset)** | ketamine, tiletamine | 2F-NENDCK, 2F-DCK, 2-BDCK | DCK (polite), HXE, NENK, 2-MeO-ket |
| **Compulsiveness** | ketamine (short), 2F-DCK (weak so they keep going) | 2F-NENDCK, DCK (weekend, not bump) | HXE, 2-MeO-ket, 2-BDCK |
| **Hole** | ketamine | DCK, 2F-DCK (good batch), tiletamine (ugly smash) | NENK, HXE, 2-BDCK, 2-MeO-ket |
| **Walk** | DCK, HXE, 2F-NENDCK | 2F-DCK, FXE | tiletamine (motor wreck), ketamine hole-dose |
| **Warmth** | HXE, ketamine, DCK | FXE | 2F-NENDCK (coffee), 2-MeO-ket |
| **Mania** | 2F-NENDCK (tinge), tiletamine | FXE, DCK | ketamine, NENK, 2-MeO-ket |
| **Residual** | DCK (next-day wooze), 2F-NENDCK (hangover / can’t sleep) | 2F-DCK dirty batches, FXE-as-CanKet | ketamine (short), NENK, tiletamine (pure is short) |

**Hated redose ≠ low enjoyment.** Ketamine is the textbook: they bump until speech is soup *because they like it*. 2F-DCK people bump because the bag is weak — that is compulsiveness, not love. NENK people redose into a ceiling and still call it meh.

### ketamine — ket / K / special K

Status: **well_documented**  
Market: **street**  
Headline: Kitchen-door hole. Bump until the floor wins.

The one. Short. Party / clinic / kitchen floor. Mid bumps are giggles, music, and “I love this couch.” Then the fiend: another key, another key, until speech is soup and the room is a television you fell into. Rush is a warm whoosh, not a stim snap — still the most *immediate* punch in this kit. Euphoria is real (float, afterglow, dumb joy) and not MDMA. Compulsiveness is the family legend. Enantiomer rumor (S more hole / clearer, R warmer / weirder) is the same bottle on the street. Norketamine is a metabolite, not a SKU.

### DCK — deschloroketamine / DXE / 2′-oxo-PCM / O-PCM

Status: **well_documented**  
Market: **rc_era**  
Headline: DCK is ket that stays for dinner.

Ket minus the ring chlorine. Longer, cleaner, more *do a thing* at low, still a hole if you insist. Forum love: physical + cognitive euphoria even when you can still watch a movie. Rush is late and polite. Hole is shallower than ket for a lot of people and then overstays. Compulsive, but the duration kills the bump-loop and replaces it with a *weekend* loop. ### 2F-DCK — 2-FDCK / 2-fluorodeschloroketamine / 2-FK

Status: **well_documented**  
Market: **rc_era**  
Headline: 2F-DCK batch lottery.

Ket’s fluoro cousin and the envelope you gamble on. Good shards: most people would fail a blind test vs ket, hole a little less deep, linger a little longer, a bit more confusion. Bad shards: fishy smell, brown glass that does nothing, “cringy,” a mountain of sand. Forum “R/S isomer” talk is almost always **dirt / leftover solvent**, not a chiral boutique. Enjoyment is the average of shine and trash — that is why it scores under ket, not because the molecule is cursed. Same hole-fiend as ket when the bag is real. Something is often *missing* (the hug, the defined visuals). WHO ECDD and rat work say abuse potential in the ket neighborhood. Bladder still collects.

**Hole vs walk:** Walk/wonk is the default. Hole on good shards. Dumb hole on bad ones.

### 2F-NENDCK — CanKet / 2-FXE / 2′-fluoro-2-oxo-PCE

Status: **moderately_documented**  
Market: **street** (as ket / FXE) + **rc_era**  
Headline: Late Flask sticker.

Canberra’s “new ket,” then the world’s. Same carbon count as FXE, fluorine at **2** not 3. Sold as ket, FXE, MXE, 2F-DCK, once in a while as itself. CanTEST: a fat slice of “ketamine” bags were this, 2F-DCK, or tiletamine. Feel split: some say more recreational than 2F-DCK (stim edge, visuals, functional, “life is good”); harm-redox writeups say mild-to-no euphoria, more disorient, more hangover, hours that are *def not ketamine*. NDEWS: longer/stronger ket **or** body-horror visuals and bladder/kidney scare. Compulsive because it is the current ket-shaped powder. Mania is a tinge, not 3-MeO-PCP.
