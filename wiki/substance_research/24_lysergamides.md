# Assignment 24 — Lysergamides

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a cookbook.** No synthesis. No precursors. No recipes. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. Scores are unitless **0–100 or null** inferences from public reports. **Enjoyment / euphoria lead.** Rush is usually none. Compulsiveness is low. Negatives sit below.

Family: blotter lysergamides. Same mesh for the whole wall (`wiki/product_families.md`). **1P-LSD is the RC door.** Street LSD can leak earlier as finished tabs. NBOMe blotter is a different kit and a high fail-state if sold as acid — never print that sticker.

---

## Scope

**In (full cards):** LSD, 1P-LSD, ALD-52, 1cP-LSD, AL-LAD, ETH-LAD, PRO-LAD.

**In (documented RC / forensic relatives):** 1B-LSD, 1V-LSD, 1D-LSD, 1T-LSD, 1S-LSD, LSZ, 1P-ETH-LAD, 1cP-AL-LAD, MIPLA, 1cP-MIPLA, LSM-775, LSA (ergine).

**In (thin / mostly null):** 1P-AL-LAD (analytical paper; not a circulating bag when described), BU-LAD, PARGY-LAD, IP-LAD, MLD-41, LAMPA, LAE-32, DAM-57, BOL-148.

**Alias, not a second agent:** **Orange Sunshine = LSD.** Courtroom story said ALD-52. Sand and Scully later said it was LSD the whole time. Flag `rumor`, not a new ID.

**Alias, not a second agent:** **1A-LSD = ALD-52.** **1CP-LSD / 1-cyclopropanoyl-LSD = 1cP-LSD.** **Valerie = 1V-LSD.**

**Out:** opioids. Ergot medicines (ergometrine, methylergometrine, methysergide, cabergoline, lisuride) are not party SKUs. NBOMe / 2C / tryptamines live in other assignments. Do not invent extra 1-acyl stickers (1H, 1Fe, 1DP, 1BP, 1SB, furan-1F, 1Bz, 1N) just because a chart can draw them.

---

## The one rule that sorts this wall

Two stamps, two fantasies:

| Stamp | What it actually is | What the night is |
|---|---|---|
| **1-acyl on LSD** (acetyl / propionyl / butanoyl / cyclopropanoyl / valeroyl / later law stickers) | **Prodrug of LSD.** Serum and liver take the acyl off. Human 1P casework found LSD, not the parent. | **Blotter clone.** Same movie as LSD. 1P is how a generation met acid. |
| **N6 swap or amide swap** (allyl / ethyl / propyl / azetidide / morpholide / methylisopropyl) | **Active analogue.** It is not waiting to become LSD. | **Actually different** — or weaker, or a flop. |

A 1-acyl on an analogue (1P-ETH-LAD, 1cP-AL-LAD) is a **prodrug of that analogue**, not a third flavor and not an LSD clone.

**Orange Sunshine was a lawyer name for regular sunshine.** Not a secret analogue.

Duration: do not invent hours. Street rumor says AL-LAD / PRO-LAD run shorter. Shulgin listed them shorter than LSD. Global Drug Survey 2016 (Coney et al. 2017) lumped analogues and found **no significant duration or peak-time difference** vs LSD, and rated the analogue group *weaker* on pleasure and strength. Catalog keeps both facts and does not pick a fake clock.

## How this shelf feels (one board)

Rush is not a slider here. Blotter is a door that opens, not a slam. Compulsiveness stays low: the night is already long, tomorrow is already spent.

| Slider | High | Mid | Low / other |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enjoyment** | LSD, 1P, AL-LAD | ETH-LAD, LSZ, PRO-LAD | MIPLA, LSA, **LSM-775** |
| **Euphoria** | AL-LAD (warmer), LSD / 1P | ETH-LAD, LSZ | PRO-LAD, MIPLA, LSA, LSM-775 |
| **Rush** | none of them | body-energy rumor on a hard come-up | **family default: none** |
| **Compulsiveness** | still low; “ate another because I thought it was weak” | LSD / 1P | the rest |
| **Visual** | ETH-LAD, AL-LAD, LSD | LSZ (geometric), PRO-LAD | MIPLA, LSA, LSM-775 |
| **Headspace weight** | LSD, 1P, ETH-LAD | LSZ | **AL-LAD** (friendlier), PRO-LAD, LSM-775 |
| **Body load** | LSA, LSM-775, some ETH-LAD | LSZ | AL-LAD, clean LSD / 1P |

**Clone rack (do not print as different trips):** LSD ← 1P-LSD, ALD-52, 1cP-LSD, 1B-LSD, 1V-LSD, 1D-LSD, 1T-LSD, 1S-LSD.

**Actually different:** AL-LAD, ETH-LAD, PRO-LAD, LSZ, MIPLA, LSM-775, LSA.

**Analogue prodrugs (feel like the parent, not like 1P):** 1P-ETH-LAD → ETH-LAD. 1cP-AL-LAD → probably AL-LAD. 1cP-MIPLA → probably MIPLA.

## LSD (LSD-25, acid, tabs, sunshine)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: The night that teaches you what a night is.

### The hit

Rush **6** (A) — none. A slow door. Some people call the come-up “energy.” That is not a rush.

Euphoria **80** (A) — mood lift, wonder, music, the grin. Not a roll. Not MDMA. The joy is the head opening, not a body slam.

Enjoyment **92** (A) — the gold-standard blotter. People write sermons. People also have bad nights. The *want* to do it again is about the experience, not a binge loop.

Compulsiveness **18** (A) — low. The night is already the whole night. Instant tolerance kills the encore. The fail-state is “I thought the tab was weak,” not redose-hell.

### The bill

Anxiety and a heavy head if the room is wrong. Sleep is gone until it is over. Next day can be flat or holy; both show up. Mild body tightness. Psychosis is a real fail-state for the wrong brain, not the typical receipt. The deadly blotter story on this island is **NBOMe sold as acid** — different kit, same paper.

NPC line: "The night that teaches you what a night is."

Flags: `mislabel` (NBOMe / research-chem tabs stamped as acid)

Sources: Hofmann history; modern 5-HT2A clinical work; Coney 2017 GDS; house feel-card.

Gaps: street tabs are a lottery (underdosed, leftover 1P, NBOMe). Catalog cannot score a random festival square as pure LSD.

## 1P-LSD (1-propionyl-LSD)

Status: well_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: 1P is the RC door. Same movie, legal envelope.

### The hit

Rush **6** (A) — none. Same door as LSD.

Euphoria **78** (A) — LSD’s mood. Some write a slightly smoother/slower open (hydrolysis gossip). Blind tests in forums mostly fail.

Enjoyment **90** (A) — this is how the RC generation met acid. Brandt / Halberstadt: hydrolyzes to LSD; rat plasma fills with LSD; a human 1P blotter case found LSD in serum and urine, not the parent. Weak 5-HT2A as the intact acyl. The trip is the metabolite.

Compulsiveness **16** (A) — low. Same “maybe the tab is weak” mistake.

### The bill

Same bill as LSD. Dirty clone blotters (iso-1P, leftover LSD) showed up when second-rate labs copied the print. That is a `mislabel` / quality roll, not a new feel.

NPC line: "1P is the RC door. Same movie, legal envelope."

Flags: `mislabel` (cheap copies; sometimes a pinch of LSD in the paper)

Sources: Brandt et al. *Return of the lysergamides* Part I (2016); Halberstadt / Brandt 1-acyl prodrug paper (PMC9191647); Grumann 2019 intoxication; EMCDDA first detect 2015.

Gaps: no licensed human psychopharm head-to-head vs LSD. “Smoother come-up” stays rumor.

### Which analogues actually feel different vs blotter clones

**Blotter clones (active compound is LSD):**

1P-LSD, ALD-52, 1cP-LSD, 1B-LSD, 1V-LSD, and — by class inference, not unique reports — 1D-LSD, 1T-LSD, 1S-LSD.

**Actually different (active analogue, not an LSD prodrug):**

| SKU | Difference vs LSD | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **AL-LAD** | Friendlier, warmer, more visual, less sinister / less mindfuck. The one that earns a second bottle. | B |
| **ETH-LAD** | Pushier / more visual, sometimes more body. Not a 1P. Polarizing. | B |
| **PRO-LAD** | Picnic, clearer, less “LSD flavor.” Rare and bait-prone. | C |
| **LSZ** | LSD-shaped, tighter/geometric, a bit more body. | C |
| **MIPLA** | Quieter, weaker, twisted amide. | D |
| **LSM-775** | Weak, nauseous, sedating. 1A brake. Flop. | B |
| **LSA** | Seed night. Body load, thin visuals. Not blotter. | B |

**Analogue prodrugs (different from 1P because the metabolite is not LSD):**

1P-ETH-LAD → ETH-LAD (shown). 1cP-AL-LAD → probably AL-LAD (not shown in the sheet paper). 1cP-MIPLA → probably MIPLA.

**Rumor, not a molecule:** Orange Sunshine as ALD-52.

**v1 board (what a dealer actually stocks):** LSD (street), **1P-LSD (RC door)**, 1cP-LSD / 1V-LSD as later doors, **AL-LAD** and **ETH-LAD** as the two extras that are worth a second art file. PRO-LAD / LSZ / LSM-775 are gray crate. Do not print a 1-acyl zoo in the UI.

## Gaps

- No licensed human head-to-head of LSD vs 1P vs AL-LAD vs ETH-LAD with confirmed paper.
- AL-LAD “shorter” vs Coney 2017 “same clock as a group” is unresolved. Do not invent hours.
- 1cP vs 1P hydrolysis speed in people is not a published number.
- 1D / 1T / 1S unique feel is unevidenced; 1D bags may be 1T.
- 1cP-AL-LAD / 1cP-MIPLA deacylation is expected, not proven in the forensic note.
- PRO-LAD modern supply is contaminated by scam listings.
- Dirty isomer blotters (iso-1P, cis-LSZ) confuse “this analogue feels dirty.”
- NBOMe-as-acid deaths will be blamed on “LSD” in NPC talk. That is a `mislabel` roll on the paper, not this family’s tox.
- Seed LSA reports are never pure ergine.
- Later silicon / phosphoryl / ferrocene stickers: names exist on charts; do not add SKUs without a seizure or a paper.
