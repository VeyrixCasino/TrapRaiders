# Duration note 07 — Review / forensic / harm-reduction clocks

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a cookbook.** No recipes, precursors, yields, glassware, or how-to-take. No dose tables. Reviews are **weak supporting evidence**. Forum cards still own the feel; this file only asks what published summaries said about **duration of action**.

**Duration ≠ HalfLife.** Gameplay `D` is the pleasurable fun window in minutes. Residual wake, ED intoxication, locomotor hours, blood/urine detectability, and clinical t½ are **not** Duration. If a paper only has a half-life, it lives in a HalfLife note below.

Locked reference: **α-PVP Duration = 90**. Do not move it. This note does **not** edit `GAMEPLAY_STATS_*`.

Question this file answers: should MDPV gameplay `D` sit at **~2×, 3×, or more** versus that 90?

---

## How to read a review clock

Most “duration of action” sentences in this shelf are **recycled user tables**, not controlled human PK. EMCDDA, WHO, Coppola, TripSit, checkit!, and PsychonautWiki all lean on the same Erowid-shaped oral / nasal envelope. That is still useful as a **weak** check. It is not a reason to copy 7 hours into `D`.

Split every quote into one of three buckets:

| Bucket | What it is | Gameplay field |
|---|---|---|
| **Desired / subjective / euphoric / plateau** | The fun window | **Duration** |
| **Total time still up / after-effects / insomnia / ED toxidrome** | Residual stim | **HalfLife** (gameplay_estimate), never D |
| **Plasma / urine t½, brain/serum ratio, detectability** | Elimination or assay | **HalfLife note only** |

Redose confounds almost every human clock. Erowid says the first MDPV pass can look like an hour and later passes stretch the night. Reviews that print “2–7 hours” are printing that mess, not a single-hit fun window.

## MDPV — what reviews actually said (Duration)

These mention **duration of action / desired effects / subjective high**. Not synthesis. Not a dose chart.

| Source | Kind | Duration language | Keep as D? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EMCDDA / EUDA risk assessment 2014** (and the Irish RAR annex) | Agency review | Onset of **desired** effects 5–30 min. Desired effects **2–7 h** oral and nasal. No formal human PK; they reprint the Erowid table (oral total 2.0–7.0 h, plateau 30–180 min; insufflated total 2.0–3.5 h, plateau 30–100 min). After-effects 2–48 h oral / 1–7 days nasal sit **off** this clock. Also: MDPV looks **longer-lasting than cocaine** in animals; clinical reports “appear consistent.” | Desired 2–7 h is the whole envelope. Plateau / insufflated total is the fun band. After-effects are HalfLife. |
| **DEA diversion fact sheet** (quoted by state labs) | Forensic / agency | **Subjective** effects about **2–3 h**. Adverse effects **6–8 h**. | 2–3 h is D. 6–8 h is HalfLife. |
| **Penders & Gestring 2011 / Penders 2012 *WestJEM* “Ivory Wave of Trouble”** | Clinical review | User reports: **euphoric high 2–4 h**, prominent letdown several hours after. | 2–4 h is D. Letdown is the bill. |
| **Gershman & Fass 2012 *P&T*** | Clinical review | Lumped bath-salt (MDPV / mephedrone / methylone) **stimulant effects ~3–4 h**. | Weak, mixed SKUs. Still a 3–4 h stim clock, not a 6–8 h tox clock. |
| **Coppola & Mondola 2012 *Toxicol Lett*** (MDPV paper) | Review | Recycles Erowid: oral **2.0–7.0 h**, nasal **2.0–3.5 h**. | Same table as EMCDDA. Weak, but it is duration-of-action language. |
| **Horsley et al. 2018 *Front. Psychiatry*** | Rat paper that opens with a human-use review sentence | Recreational users: onset ~30 min, duration **120–210 min**, after-effects up to **8 h**. | 120–210 min is the cleanest single D band in the academic pile. 8 h is HalfLife. |
| **PsychonautWiki MDPV** | Harm-reduction summary | Flags “**unusually long duration**.” Oral total **2–7 h**, peak **1–4 h**, offset 0.5–2 h, after 2–48 h. | Peak 1–4 h is D. After is HalfLife. “Unusually long” is vs other stims / vs the PVP bang, not vs meth. |
| **TripSit MDPV** | Harm-reduction factsheet | Oral total **2–7 h**. Insufflated total **2–3.5 h** (wiki) or **2–4 h** (factsheet). After 2–48 h. | Same Erowid spine. Fun window is the 2–4 h nasal / peak, not the 48 h hang. |
| **checkit! Vienna MDPV card** | Drug-checking / HR | Nasal 5–20 min, oral 15–30 min. Effect **2–7 h**, oral longer. Redose “prolongs the effect disproportionately.” After-effects up to **48 h**. | Same envelope. Redose warning is why 7 h is not a single-hit D. |
| **WHO ECDD MDPV critical review** | Agency review | Human duration is thin. Animal side: Bonano ICSS “**extraordinarily long duration of action**” vs cocaine; Eshleman / Gatch locomotor **5–6 h** in mice; Baumann microdialysis **longer-lasting than cocaine**. | Animal locomotor / DAT occupancy ≠ fun window. Use as “longer than coke,” not as a 5–6 h D. |

**MDPV Duration cluster (reviews only):** desired / subjective / euphoric high sits around **2–4 hours** (120–240 min). The 2–3 h DEA line, the 120–210 min Horsley line, the Erowid/TripSit insufflated 2–3.5 h, and the Penders 2–4 h euphoric high all point at the same band. The EMCDDA / PW **2–7 h** is the **oral total + redose** ceiling, not the fun window.

## α-PVP — what reviews actually said (Duration)

Locked gameplay D is already **90**. These quotes are only here to check that 90 is a fun window, and to give MDPV something to be “way longer” than.

| Source | Kind | Duration language | Keep as D? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EMCDDA / EUDA α-PVP risk assessment 2015–16** | Agency review | Finnish forum: re-dose after **30–120 min** because “the effects don’t last very long,” sometimes **as short as 15 min**. Animal locomotor: Aarde rats peak ~2 h; Gatch mice 2.5–10 mg/kg stimulant effect **240–290 min**. | The 15–120 min re-dose clock is the street fun window. 4–5 h mouse locomotor is leftover stim. |
| **I-TREND / OFDT α-PVP briefing** (Polish forums) | Forensic / market briefing | Total described effects **1–10 h**. Psychological plateau **30–120 min**, then the glow drops and people re-dose for another 1–2 h of stimulation. Physical effects last much longer. Coming-down **5–10 h**. One user: α-PVP is “**3 times weaker and shorter**” than MDPV. | 30–120 min psych is D. 5–10 h comedown is HalfLife. The “3× shorter” line is **one forum voice** inside a briefing — weak, and it mixes potency with clock. |
| **Nóbrega & Dinis-Oliveira 2018 *Drug Metab Rev*** / **Karila “twisted sisters” 2018 *Neuropharmacology*** / **Patocka “Flakka” 2020** | Forensic / clinical reviews | Human psychopharmacology: effects ~10 min after a single pass, peak **10–40 min**, lasting **2–3 h**. Karila-class reviews also say “multiple hours” without a tight split. Nóbrega is mostly tox / metabolites, not a fun clock. | 2–3 h is an **oral / unspecified** total, not the vape bang. Peak 10–40 min is why the house 90 is already generous. |
| **PsychonautWiki α-PVP** | Harm-reduction summary | **Smoked total 30–60 min** (peak 3–6 min). Oral total 2–6 h. Insufflated total 2–5 h. After-effects 1–12 h by route. | Smoked 30–60 is the vape-era fun window the designer already padded to 90. Oral/IN totals are not that 90. |
| **De la Rosa / Papaseit 2025 *Front. Pharmacol.*** | Naturalistic human PD (n=9, IN) | IN: literature says ~**3 h**, re-dose 30–120 min; oral up to **6 h** (citing Stanciu). Observed: onset 20 min, peak **40 min–1 h**, most subjective effects **nearly gone by 5 h**, some gone by 3 h. | Closest thing to measured human fun. Still a small n, still IN, still not a reason to move the locked 90. The 3–5 h fade includes the come-down shoulder. |

**α-PVP Duration cluster (reviews only):** the **pleasurable** window is short. Psychological 30–120 min (I-TREND), vape 30–60 min (PW), agency re-dose at 30–120 min, sometimes 15 min. Oral/IN “2–3 h” / “up to 6 h” and the 2025 “gone by 3–5 h” are **total subjective**, not the bang the 90 is modeling. Residual insomnia is already the house HalfLife 140, not a reason to grow D.

That is why 90 stands: it is a **generous fun window** on a short pyro, not an Erowid oral-total.

## Head-to-head (still weak)

Very few reviews put both clocks on one page and mean it.

- **I-TREND:** users treat α-PVP as the MDPV substitute. Desired effects “much weaker and last shorter.” One person said **3× weaker and shorter**. Another said MDPV needed a much smaller amount. That is a **single-thread ratio**, not a measurement.
- **Karila “twisted sisters”:** both last “multiple hours.” No usable ratio.
- **Glennon & Young 2016 *Brain Res Bull*:** animal locomotor, not human fun. Fuwa mice: MDPV motor bump ~**90 min** then a drop. Gatch mice: α-PVP **4–5 h**. If you believed locomotor hours you would **invert** the street story. Do not.
- **PsychonautWiki:** MDPV is the one they call “unusually long.” α-PVP smoked is a 30–60 min card. Oral-total tables (MDPV 2–7 vs α-PVP 2–6) look almost equal and **must not** be used as the ratio. The house 90 is not PW’s oral total.

Naive table math fails on purpose:

| Bad comparison | Fake ratio | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| MDPV oral total 2–7 h vs α-PVP smoked 30–60 min | 3–7× | Different routes, different clocks. |
| MDPV oral total vs α-PVP oral total | ~1× | Both are leftover-inclusive totals. House PVP D is not that. |
| I-TREND “3× shorter” | 3× | One user. Mixes strength and time. |
| Mouse locomotor 5–6 h vs 4–5 h | ~1× or inverted | Not a fun window. |
| Ross “intoxication 6–8 h” vs PVP 90 | 4–5× | Tox clock. HalfLife. |

The fair review comparison is **MDPV desired/subjective 2–4 h** versus **α-PVP psych / vape bang 0.5–2 h**, with the house already sitting PVP at **90** (top of that bang, not the 15-minute Finnish line).

## HalfLife notes (not Duration)

Put these on the decay / residual card. **Do not copy them into D.**

### MDPV

- **Anizan / Baumann rat PK:** plasma peak 10–20 min, elimination t½ **~80 min** (Horsley cites ~80 min; Hambuchen cites racemic MDPV **~1.3 h** s.c. in SD rats). Fast drop. Not a 3-hour fun argument.
- **Horsley 2018 rats:** tissue peaks <30 min; brain/serum ratio ~2 for ~**120 min**. That 120 min tracks residual presence, not pleasure.
- **WHO / Eshleman / Gatch:** mouse locomotor **5–6 h**. Long DAT dwell vs cocaine (Bonano). Residual stim / reinforcement length, not D.
- **DEA:** adverse **6–8 h**.
- **Ross et al. 2012 *Am J Med*:** “the intoxication lasts **6–8 hours**.” Hospital toxidrome. Same clock as DEA adverse. **Not** the euphoric high.
- **Erowid / TripSit / EMCDDA table:** after-effects **2–48 h** (oral) or **1–7 days** (nasal). Insomnia after the plateau. HalfLife / aftereffects, never D.
- No clean published **human** elimination t½ for MDPV turned up in this pass. Do not invent one.

### α-PVP

- **Namera et al. 2013 *Forensic Toxicol*:** one human IV case. Urinary excretion t½ **~22 h** (later window ~40 h). Assay / elimination. Useless as D.
- **Hambuchen et al. 2021 rats:** serum terminal t½ **~2.1 h**, **62% longer than MDPV 1.3 h** in the same design. If you copied t½ into Duration you would make PVP the long one and **fail the house rule**. This is the teaching example for Duration ≠ HalfLife.
- **Rojek 2016 traffic-accident case:** α-PVP still in blood **~13.5 h** after insufflation (low tens of ng/mL). Detectability, not a high.
- **I-TREND / PW after-effects:** 5–10 h come-down, insomnia “cannot be slept off,” PW after 1–12 h. House HalfLife **140** already lives here.
- **Gatch 2015 mice:** locomotor **4–5 h**. Same bucket as MDPV’s 5–6 h mouse hours — leftover, not D.

**PK paradox (keep this sentence):** rat t½ says **α-PVP ≥ MDPV**. Street / HR fun windows say **MDPV lasts way longer**. Gameplay D follows the fun window. Gameplay HalfLife may follow residual wake, not the rat number.

## Recommendation

**MDPV gameplay Duration should be ~2× α-PVP 90, not 3×, and not more.**

| Option | Minutes if PVP = 90 | What that would be stealing | Verdict |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **~2×** | **~180** | DEA subjective 2–3 h, Horsley 120–210, Penders euphoric 2–4 h, Erowid/TripSit nasal 2–3.5 h, PW peak 1–4 h | **Use this.** Fun window. Substantially > 90. Matches “way longer” without eating the comedown. |
| 3× | 270 | Mid–upper EMCDDA 2–7 h, I-TREND one-user “3×,” oral-total shoulder | Too much leftover stim in D. Reviews do not earn this. |
| More | 360+ | Ross/DEA 6–8 h intoxication, 2–48 h after, mouse 5–6 h, redose nights | HalfLife / aftereffects. Fail. |

Why **2×** and not 3×:

1. House `D` is the **fun window**. The review sentences that actually say “subjective,” “desired,” “euphoric high,” or “plateau” cluster at **2–4 hours**. 180 sits in the middle.
2. The 7-hour and 6–8-hour clocks are **oral-total, redose, or hospital intoxication**. Those are the same class of leftover the house already refused to pour into α-PVP’s 90 (forum bang is shorter; wake is HalfLife 140).
3. Reviews are **weak**. They reprint Erowid. One I-TREND user saying “3 times shorter” is not a multiplier.
4. α-PVP 90 is already a **padded** bang (PW smoked 30–60, I-TREND psych 30–120). Doubling that is a real, playable gap. Tripling it starts comparing MDPV’s leftover to PVP’s already-padded fun.
5. A 48 (or any D ≤ 90) was inverted and must not ship. **180 is substantially > 90.** That satisfies `GAMEPLAY_RESPONSE` without pretending a review measured 4.5 hours of pleasure.

**HalfLife (gameplay_estimate only):** MDPV residual wake should read **longer than 180**, not copied from the 80-minute rat t½. The 6–8 h adverse / “hours you cannot sleep” band is the hint. Do not set MDPV HalfLife = Duration. Do not set it from Anizan’s 80. α-PVP HalfLife stays a separate leftover card (house 140 is already in that neighborhood).

**Onset (optional, not a how-to):** reviews print MDPV desired onset 5–30 min (nasal faster than oral). α-PVP vape/IN is minutes. Not a Duration input.

## Gaps

- No controlled human duration study for MDPV. The 2025 α-PVP IN observational (n=9) is the only modern human PD clock, and it is the short sister.
- Stanciu 2017 is cited for oral α-PVP “up to 6 h”; treat as another recycled review sentence, not new data.
- This file did not re-score forums. If a later `_dur_*` forum card shows a longer **pleasurable** MDPV plateau than 2–4 h, it can argue the number. It still should not jump to 3× just because EMCDDA printed “up to 7 hours.”
- Animal ICSS / DAT k_off (Sitte / Gradisch 2023: slow pyro k_off vs cocaine) explains **why** pyros feel sticky. It does not pick 180 vs 270.

## Bottom line

Reviews are a **weak yes** on “MDPV lasts way longer than α-PVP.” They are a **weak no** on 3× or more. Gameplay Duration is the fun window. **Recommend MDPV D ≈ 2× the locked α-PVP 90 (~180 minutes).** Put 6–8 h intoxication, 2–48 h after, and every t½ in HalfLife, not Duration. Do not ship 48. Do not edit the stats boards from this file.
