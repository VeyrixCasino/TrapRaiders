# Assignment 05 — Methylenedioxy pyrrolidine cathinones (MD-pyros)

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a manual.** No recipes. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. No injection SOP. Scores are unitless **0–100 or null**. **Rush / euphoria / enjoyment / compulsiveness are independent** — a hated binge can still mean high compulsiveness. Unknown = `null`, not `0`. Do not invent.

Family: **3,4-methylenedioxy + pyrrolidine** on the pyrovalerone backbone (Kit H). Unsubstituted α-PXP (Kit I) is another agent. This file only owns the MD column plus the 2014 almost-MD copycats that sat on the same wall.

**Alias collisions (not extra molecules):**

## How this shelf feels (one board)

The MD stamp does **not** make a roll. These are DAT/NET blockers with a little more SERT than the naked α-PXP twins — still NDRI monsters, not MDMA. Longer tail = stronger DAT on paper until C7, where the street says the fun dies and the side effects stay.

| Slider | High | Mid | Low / empty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rush** | MDPV | MDPHP, MDPiHP (smoked myth), 5-DBFPV | MDPPP (useful, not a slam poem), **MDPEP** (almost nothing) |
| **Euphoria** | MDPV, 5-DBFPV (2014) | MDPHP, MDPBP (tan rumor) | MDPPP (coke-y background), MDPEP |
| **Enjoyment** | MDPPP (old heads: *more useful than MDPV*), 5-DBFPV | MDPHP, MDPV (love/hate) | MDPiHP (wreck reports), **MDPEP** |
| **Compulsiveness** | **MDPV**, then MDPiHP / MDPHP | 5-DBFPV, MDPBP | **MDPPP** (the point of the old-head take), MDPEP (weak, still sticky for some) |
| **Pleasure ≠ fiend** | MDPPP can be liked more *and* redosed less | MDPV is both gorgeous and a trap | MDPEP is neither |

That split is the point. MDPV is the Ivory Wave bath-salt monster. MDPHP is the current workhorse. 5-DBFPV is 2014 almost-MD. MDPPP is the short one old heads sometimes liked more than MDPV.

### MDPV — 3,4-methylenedioxypyrovalerone / MD-α-PVP / Ivory Wave

Status: **well_documented**  
Market: **street**  
Name status: **market-confirmed**  
Pair: α-PVP (C5)

The one that made the news. Bath-salt / Ivory Wave / Super Coke. EMCDDA–Europol Joint Report + 2014 risk assessment. First EU notification Finland 2008. Tokyo street mention 2007. EU: 107 non-fatal + 99 deaths 2008–2013, >200 kg seized that window. STRIDA Sweden 2010–2014: 201 confirmed intoxications, peak then crash to almost zero after control. Baumann 2013: powerful cocaine-like DAT/NET blocker, not a releaser. Gannon 2018: ~10× more potent and ~3× more effective reinforcer than cocaine in rats; DAT/SERT selectivity ~750. PsychonautWiki subjective: stimulation, disinhibition, libido, powerful euphoria — and compulsive redose, psychosis, mania at a higher rate than other stims. MD ring does **not** make Molly. Mild entactogen rumor only.

Status: **well_documented**  
Market: **street** (current MD workhorse)  
Name status: **market-confirmed**  
Pair: α-PHP (C6)

Kolaczynska 2021: DAT/NET blocker, DAT/SERT 80–241 band for the MD series. Street: still a pyro, **not a roll**. Slightly milder / less fiendish than MDPV in the talk pages — still the workhorse.

## Gaps

- No head-to-head human study of MDPV vs MDPHP vs MDPiHP. Rankings are seizure mass + forum weather + rodent DAT.
- MDPHP vs MDPV “milder / less compulsive” is talk-page consensus, not a trial. ED toxidromes look the same.
- MDPHP / MDPiHP chromatographic mix-ups mean some “MDPHP” case counts may be the iso twin.
- MDPEP: elite in-vitro DAT, dead on the street. Unresolved. Do not “fix” the street scores to flatter the assay.
- MDPOP: RESPONSE 1716-16 not independently read here (paywall/catalog). Treat market as **uncertain**, not vendor-confirmed flood.
- 5-DBFPV has **no** published DAT/NET/SERT table in the sources pulled. Cayman says properties unknown.
- 2,3-MDPV: no confirmed street seizure of the 2,3 isomer found.
- MD-PV10 / 6-DBFPV / 5-DBFPHP / MD-PCYP: not confirmed. Leave off the board.
- House `slam_only?` on MDPPP is **wrong-shaped**. Do not ship that flag.
- Ivory Wave / NRG-1 / monkey dust bags were mixed and mislabeled. NPC heat should roll `mislabel`.
- CAS collisions on MDPHP and MDPEP. Print both, do not invent a merge.
- No doses, no half-lives invented. Duration chatter stayed qualitative.
