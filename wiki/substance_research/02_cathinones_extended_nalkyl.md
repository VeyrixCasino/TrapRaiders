# Assignment 02 — Extended-chain and N-substituted cathinones

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a cookbook.** No recipes, precursors, yields, glassware, doses-as-how-to, or injection SOP. Scores are **coke = 10**, uncapped (house stim ruler in `CALIBRATION.md` / `FEEL_GRAPH.md`). Hexen is diet (~6 glow, 20 compulsive). NEP is 5 / 7 / 11 / 32. 4-MMC is 24 euphoria — **not on this wall**. **Rush / euphoria / enjoyment / compulsiveness are scored independently** — a hated binge can still mean high compulsiveness. Unknown = `null`, never `0`.

Family: synthetic cathinones on Kit E — **longer α-chain** (buphedrone → pentedrone → hexedrone → heptedrone) and **N-alkyl / N-ethyl stamps** (MEC / EEC / CEC / EMC). Not MMC/CMC/FMC/BMC (assignment 01). Not pyros (04–06). Not bk-MDMA (03). Not methedrone (01 / Kit G).

Vendor packet names are **aliases or blends**, not extra molecules. `NRG-2` is usually 4-MEC. A bag stamped `4-MPD` was sometimes 4-MEAP. `Hexen` is N-ethylhexedrone, not hexedrone.

## How this wall feels (one board)

Longer α-chain = less hug, more NDRI grind, more residual wake. N-ethyl on a long chain (NEP, hexen) is the **fiend stamp**. Ring-ethyl / ring-chloro on a short chain (EEC / CEC) is the **thinner copy**. Mexedrone put a methoxy on the chain and the magic fell out.

| Slider | High | Mid | Low / empty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enjoyment** | NEP | hexen (first night), 4-EMC, 4-MEAP, NEB | 4-CEC, mexedrone, 3-MEC |
| **Euphoria** | 4-EMC (rumor), 3,4-DMMC (old split) | NEP social-cold, hexen first bump | pentedrone, 4-MEC after the spike, mexedrone |
| **Rush** | **hexen** first / vape talk | 4-MEC onset panic-rush, NEP | hexedrone, mexedrone |
| **Compulsiveness** | **hexen**, **NEP** | 4-MEC, NEB | mexedrone (nothing to chase) |
| **Duration of the high** | NEP (accumulates; next-day wake) | hexen shorter, pentedrone functional | 4-MEC “fifteen minutes then bill” |
| **Hug / serotonin rumor** | 4-MEC / 4-EMC / 3,4-DMMC (weak) | NEB “tinge” | NEP / hexen / pentedrone — **cold** |
| **Workability** | pentedrone, low NEP | 4-MEC “you can go to work” | hexen binge, 4-CEC |

## NEP (N-ethylpentedrone, ethyl-pentedrone, N-ethyl-nor-pentedrone, α-EAPP)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: If it lasts till noon, it wasn’t 3-MMC.

### The hit
- **Rush 5 / B** — House diet-coke snap, under hexen’s punch. Smoother than hexen. Saferparty: coke/amp mix, not a roll.
- **Euphoria 7 / B** — Mood-up and talk, **no hug**. Checking services call it cold. Not 4-MMC’s 24.
- **Enjoyment 11 / A** — The one people keep after hexen.
- **Compulsiveness 32 / A** — House number. 3-MMC-mislabel habit; hated-but-36h. Not pyro 120. Wake accumulates. Hated next-day ≠ low enjoyment last night.

### The bill
Paranoia / residual stim / chest-tight rumor after binges. Sleep is the tax. Comedown often called *smoother than hexen*, still irritable and flat. Sold as 3-MMC / 4-MMC across CH / FR / DE / Berlin (DIZ: since Apr 2025, **no** declared 3-MMC sample was 3-MMC).

NPC line: “If it lasts till noon, it wasn’t 3-MMC.”  
route_myth: `mislabel` `redose` `paranoia`

## N-ethylhexedrone (hexen, HEX-EN, Hex-en, NEH, ethyl-hexedrone)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: NEP’s little brother who still steals.

### The hit
- **Rush 6 / B** — House diet-coke first bump. Why it sold. PsychonautWiki: initial rush that **does not come back**. Slam talk is a flag, not a SOP.
- **Euphoria 6 / B** — Front-loaded. Third redose is awake and empty.
- **Enjoyment 7 / B** — 2016–18 favorite, then a lot of people switched to NEP. First night can feel like a 10. The bag is a 4.
- **Compulsiveness 20 / A** — House greedy. Below NEP’s mislabel 32, far below PVP 120.

### The bill
Harsher on the nose than NEP (blood/mucus reports). Paranoia, eti-to-sleep posts. Comedown worse than NEP. EU most-seized cathinone ~2017; DEA common ID 2018. Patented 1964 as an anorectic idea; street wave late 2015.

NPC line: “NEP’s little brother who still steals.”  
route_myth: `redose` `vape_fav` `paranoia`

Sources:
- PsychonautWiki N-Ethylhexedrone (EMCDDA 2015–16 IDs; 2017 seizure rank)
- Saferparty NEP/NEH
- r/researchchemicals NEP vs Hexen (2018); “which do you like” (2018)
- Bluelight Hex-En / Ethyl-Hexedrone threads
- US Federal Register 2019 temporary Schedule I (with 4-MEAP, α-PHP, PV8)

Gaps: Hexedrone (N-methyl parent) reports are thin and get collapsed into hexen. Do not merge the two IDs.

## Pentedrone (α-methylaminovalerophenone)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: Coffee with a criminal record.

### The hit
- **Rush 4 / B** — House: functional snap, not a poem. Bluelight: “very stimmy but with no euphoria.”
- **Euphoria 3 / B** — WHO ECDD notes some users claimed MDMA-like sociability. Forum majority says **no roll**. Believe the forums for feel; the questionnaire is the outlier.

### The bill
UN Schedule II (38th ECDD 2016). Hundreds of kg seized in the EU. In “Synthacaine” and bath-salt mixes. Fatalities always polydrug. Cold hands, wake, crash.

NPC line: “Coffee with a criminal record.”

## 4-MEC (4-methylethcathinone)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: You can go to work. You will hate work.

### The hit
- **Rush 8 / B** — House: empty onset spike. Erowid: intense onset, “did I overdose,” then **nothing**. The rush is real. The stay is not.
