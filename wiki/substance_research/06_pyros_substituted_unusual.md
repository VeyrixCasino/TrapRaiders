# Assignment 06 — Substituted / unusual pyrovalerones

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a cook book.** No recipes. No precursors. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. No injection SOP.

Scores are unitless **0–100 or `null`**. Lead every card with **rush / euphoria / compulsiveness / enjoyment**. Negatives sit below. A hated binge can still score high compulsiveness. Do not average “never again” into low euphoria. Unknown = `null`, never `0`.

**This file owns Kits I-old and J, plus the documented ring stamps on the PVP / PHP / PiHP end.** It does **not** redo the plain α-PPP…PV-9 wall or the MDPPP…MDPEP wall (other agents).

## How this shelf feels (one board)

These are **stamps on the pyro chassis**, not a new family. The parent series is the 1960s medicine wall. The halogen / methyl PiHP stickers are the later EU flood. Kit J is the easter-egg crate.

| Slider | High / loud | Mid | Low / thin |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rush** | 3F-α-PVP, 3F-PiHP (diet-PiHP snap) | pyrovalerone, MPHP, 4F-α-PVP (some vape) | **4-MePPP** (“not the rush”), 4F-α-PHP, 4Cl-α-PVP, genuine naphyrone |
| **Euphoria** | 3F-α-PVP (best of the fluoro-PVP stamps) | pyrovalerone, 4F-α-PVP (split) | 4-MePPP (distant coke, no ego), 4Cl-α-PVP, naphyrone-as-labeled |
| **Enjoyment** | 3F-PiHP “not a bad pyro”; 3F-α-PVP | 4F-α-PVP (work-stim camp) | 4Cl-α-PVP, 4F-α-PHP, NRG-1 hangover |
| **Compulsiveness** | **3F-PiHP** (forum: as greedy as PiHP), 4F-PiHP | 4F-α-PVP leftover-stim redose, pyrovalerone (withdrawn for abuse) | 4-MePPP (ceiling, not a demon) |
| **Street fame** | naphyrone **name**, 4-MePPP as the weak German parent | 4F-α-PVP (STRIDA named it out loud) | TH-PVP / 5-PPDI / 5-BPDI / α-PBT (lab stickers) |

**Infer, do not invent:** naphyrone = the name on the NRG-1 packet, often MDPV in the bag. 4-MePPP = not the rush. 4F-PVP = vape talk, then an argument about whether it was worth the taste.

## 4-MePPP (MPPP, MePPP, 4-Me-α-PPP)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: Not the rush. Distant coke, then a ceiling.

### The hit

- **Rush 28 / C** — Bluelight NMR-vendor thread: a line gives a rush, “albeit not a strong one,” then 1–2 h of polite stim. That is the whole poem. House card already said it.
- **Euphoria 32 / B** — “Distant coke-high without the ego-boost.” Nuke: not stimulating or euphoric enough to get popular. A few friends called it one of the nicer cathinones. Majority: mellow, subtle, ceiling.
- **Enjoyment 38 / B** — Short, talky-enough, fewer MDPV-style body-taxes in that first wave. Still “nothing too special.”
- **Compulsiveness 30 / B** — Redose happens because it is short, not because it steals the night. People hit a ceiling and went to sleep. Hated binge is not the story here.

### The bill

Paranoia low. Aftereffects mild vs the flakka wall. Psychosis not the rumor. Sleep actually possible in the same thread. Name collision with the opioid MPPP is the real fail-state (`mislabel` on the letters, not the powder).

STRIDA saw it twice (2011–12), both times with MDPV in the same urine. German late-90s / early-00s designer-drug wave with the rest of the 4-Me wall. NRG-3 ingredient in some grey-market blends. Meltzer / Niello: less DAT-potent than pyrovalerone and MPHP; no conditioned place preference in rats; “lower abuse potential” vs the naked α-pyros. That matches the forum weather.

NPC line: “Not the rush.”  
Flags: `mislabel` (MPPP letters)  
Gaps: almost no modern trip volume; two STRIDA hits are poly-drug.

## pyrovalerone (Centroton, Thymergix, O-2371, 4-Me-α-PVP)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: street *(old pharmacy)* / rc_era  
Headline: The actual parent. A 1960s fatigue pill that got pulled for liking it too much.

### The hit

- **Rush 42 / D** — Clinical stim, not slam poetry. Nobody in the old medicine papers is writing about a snap. Infer mid, not flakka.
- **Euphoria 48 / C** — Enough that Spain / France sold Centroton / Thymergix for lethargy and appetite, then withdrew it around 2001 for abuse and dependence. Gardos & Cole 1971: chronically fatigued volunteers, it did the stimulant job.
- **Enjoyment 50 / C** — Medicine-cabinet lore. Milder than flakka in every house note and every SAR paragraph. Still a DAT/NET blocker in the same family.
- **Compulsiveness 58 / C** — Withdrawn for abuse. Withdrawal depression is in the monograph. That is not low reinforcement just because it is “old” and “milder.”

### The bill

Appetite down, anxiety, fragmented sleep, tremor. Depression on the way out. Not the zombie-headline molecule — that was MDPV / α-PVP, the children.

**Alias rule:** Kit I’s “4-Me-α-PVP” stamp **is this bottle**. Do not print a second SKU. α-PiHP is not 4-Me-PVP no matter what a 2017 thread nicknamed it.

NPC line: “Medicine-cabinet lore. The parent, not the panic.”  
Flags: —  
Gaps: almost no modern RC trip reports under the medicine name; later “4-Me-PVP” chatter is contaminated by PiHP mislabel.

## MPHP (PV-4, 4-Me-α-PHP)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: Longer Centroton. The one with the liver-and-muscle paper.

### The hit

- **Rush 44 / D** — Presumed pyro-shaped. Sauer’s man thought he had cocaine. That tells you the *intent*, not the snap.
- **Euphoria 46 / D** — Niello: MPHP sits with pyrovalerone as a potent DAT/NET blocker, well above 4-MePPP. Forum volume is thin.
- **Enjoyment 32 / C** — r/researchchemicals replacement threads: don’t go for MPHP, and don’t confuse it with MDPHP. Effects “not as good” as α-PHP / α-PiHP / 3F-PVP. Enjoyment is the retrospective score, not the DAT IC50.
- **Compulsiveness 52 / C** — Still a 4-Me hexano pyro. People bought it. Japan found it in herbs / bath salts / “liquid aroma.” DEA temporary Schedule I with 4Cl-α-PVP and PV-8 (2019). Compulsive enough to be a product. Not the flakka siren in the writeups we have.

### The bill

Sauer 2011 (Jena): agitated, jumped from a window, bilateral foot fractures, **rhabdomyolysis**, hemodialysis, toxic liver injury. Powder was MPHP, sold as cocaine. Japanese autopsy later quantified it in urine. That is the bill, and it is loud, and it is **not** proof the high was bad. Hated aftermath ≠ low euphoria — we just do not have the euphoria quotes.

NPC line: “Not MDPHP. The 4-methyl one. The paper is why old heads flinch.”  
Flags: `mislabel` (sold as coke; confused with MDPHP)  
Gaps: almost no clean “I liked this” corpus.

## 4F-α-PVP (4F-PVP, FPVP, O-2370)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: Fluoro flakka. Vape talk. Cleaner stim, less poem.

### The hit

- **Rush 38 / B** — Some IN “hits quickly.” Vape camp: mediocre, dull, disgusting taste vs α-PVP. Not the PVP snap. STRIDA patients named this one out loud (5 consults, analytics matched).
- **Euphoria 42 / B** — Split. Vaya (Bluelight): 85% NRI / 15% DRI *feeling*, recreational potential “next to none” vs α-PVP / MDPV. Juno: decent euphoria, clear head, writing fuel, diminishing returns after two or three boosters. Both can be true. Average sits under PVP.
- **Enjoyment 40 / B** — Work-stim people kept it. Rec people called it blue-balled. Longer leftover stimulation than the fun part — easy to think you came down.
- **Compulsiveness 48 / B** — Less of the empty-the-bag demon than α-PVP in the same threads. Still a redose loop on the leftover peripheral buzz. STRIDA: 19 findings, third most common “copycat.” That is a product, not a theory.

### The bill

Headache, fatigue, nose/throat burn. Adrenergic / anxiety in the 4Cl comparison thread. Vape taste is a character beat (`vape_fav` as the *attempt*, not a recommendation). Class: tachycardia, agitation — STRIDA’s shared toxidrome.

NPC line: “Later-wave sticker. They tried to vape it.”  
Flags: `vape_fav` `redose`  
Gaps: no single-substance feel study; one STRIDA mono-4F-α-PVP case exists without a published vibe paragraph.

## 2-Me-α-PiHP / 3-Me-α-PiHP / 4-Me-α-PiHP

Status: limited_evidence *(forensic)*  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: Swedish isomer walk. Blood in the file. No poems.

### The hit

**All subjective axes null / U.** Do not invent a feel from an IC50.

What we actually have (Forensic Science International 2025, Swedish NFC + RMV):

- Strong DAT-over-SERT blockers. 4-Me-α-PiHP was the most DAT-potent of the three on the bench. DAT/SERT ratio >40× cocaine for all three — that is **reinforcement-in-a-dish**, not a trip report.
- 4-Me first (2020 seizures), then 2-Me (2022), then 3-Me (2023). **113 seizures** 2020–2024. **37 forensic tox cases**, including **5 autopsies**. Heavy co-detections (amphetamines, benzos, opioids).
- 4-Me already scheduled in Sweden in 2019 and still showed up. That is a product walk, not a theory grid.

### The bill

Autopsy / DUI / violent-crime matrices exist. No substance-specific toxidrome split in the abstract. Treat as pyro harm, not as three personalities.

**Reinforcement (in-vitro, not street):** 4-Me > 3-Me > 2-Me on DAT IC50. Put that in the lab bible if you want a stamp-strength rumor. Do **not** print three enjoyment numbers.

NPC line: “Same demon, new stamp. The lab can tell 2 from 3 from 4. The UI doesn’t need all three on the shelf.”  
Flags: `redose`  
Ship rule: list all three in data. UI ships **at most those three methyls + the two fluoros**, not a poster.

## naphyrone (NRG-1, Energy-1, O-2482, naphthylpyrovalerone)

Status: identity_uncertain *(street bag)* / moderately_documented *(molecule)*  
Market: street  
Headline: The name on the packet. Often not the powder.

### The hit

Score the **molecule** when the lab said naphyrone. Score the **packet** as a lie.

- **Rush 30 / C** — Genuine-ish reports: caffeine-and-ephedrine stim, “I waited for rushes. There weren’t.” Some NRG-1 threads are mephedrone-shaped chatty rushes — those bags were often **MDPV or mephedrone**. Brandt: **1 of 10** (later 1 of 13) NRG-1 products analytically matched naphyrone. Wood / EMJ: a man ate a gram of “NRG-1” and got **butylone + MDPV**.
- **Euphoria 24 / C** — Derungs 2011 (Basel, plasma-confirmed naphyrone): restlessness, insomnia, anxiety, hallucinations for two days — not a roll. Meltzer: the rare pyro that is a true **triple** reuptake inhibitor at nM DAT/NET/SERT. On paper that could have been a hug. On the forums that hug mostly did not show.
- **Enjoyment 22 / C** — “Boring as sin.” “Can someone explain the desire to do naphyrone?” The Sun called it Europe’s crystal meth. Users who got the name on the packet and a long comedown did not write sequels.
- **Compulsiveness 42 / C** — Long, potent, easy to keep dabbing because the first hour did nothing. Not MDPV-fiend in the confirmed writeups. The **mislabel bags** inherit MDPV compulsiveness from Kit H — do not put that score on this ID.

α- vs β-naphthyl isomer: some “genuine” samples were mixed. Pharmacology in the papers is β-naphyrone. α-naphyrone is unknown. Do not ship a second SKU.

### The bill

Insomnia that laughs at the next day. Anxiety, vasoconstriction, speech glue, paranoia-in-the-club. ACMD 2010: overdose risk from potency vs mephedrone-sized lines; theoretical naphthyl carcinogen worry (that is a medicinal-chem rumor, not a street COD). Media panic sold the name.

NPC line: “The name on the packet. Not the powder.”  
Flags: `mislabel` `redose`  
Gaps: almost every 2010 “naphyrone” report is a branded product. Treat NRG-1 chatter as MDPV-shaped unless a lab is in the sentence.
