## Rush vs grind (the ranking this assignment asked for)

Rush = onset snap / flash. Not “how strong.” Not “how much they loved the night.”

### Actually have a rush

| Rank | SKU | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **methamphetamine** | The king. Smoked / slammed lore is the poem. Oral still slaps as a *high*, not the same flash. Route-gated. |
| 2 | **ethylphenidate** | The recreational phenidate. Short, coke-shaped, nose-first. Fiendish because it ends. |
| 3 | **4F-EPH** | EPH with a fluorine. Same idea, mixed “better/worse” threads. |
| 4 | **amphetamine** (insufflated / old speed-freak lore) | Real snap vs oral grind. Street paste is dirtier. Not meth. |
| 5 | **3-FA** | Closest fluoro to meth-shaped dopamine. Some rush, some mood. Rare bag. |
| 6 | **4-FMA / 4-FA** | Come-up tingle + serotonin warmth, not a coke snap. 4-FMA heavier. |
| 7 | **3,4-CTMP** | Physical slam of stimulation. That is not the same as a *fun* rush. People still describe a hit. Then they hate it. |

### Grind-only (or near)

| SKU | Why |
|---|---|
| **2-FMA** | Holy grail of functional. Little euphoria, real focus. The work fluoro. |
| **4F-MPH** | Focus monster. Some mild mood. Files taxes. Anxiety if you get greedy. Not a party. |
| **IPPH** | Smooth, mild, caffeine-plus. Forum study-drug winner. Almost no snap. |
| **methylphenidate** (oral / Concerta) | Dry focus. IR snorted has a small coke-shaped rumor; XR is homework. |
| **lisdexamfetamine** | Prodrug. Slow, long, almost no rush by design. |
| **4-Me-TMP** | Diet Ritalin. Functional leftover. |
| **phentermine** | Old diet pill. Coffee with a criminal record. |
| **MPA** | Thiophene meth. Work stim, colder, less magic than the name promises. |

### Love without a rush (do not confuse)

**4-FA** is the enjoyment king of the fluoro wall and still only a *mid* rush. **2-FMA** is enjoyed as work. **4F-MPH** is enjoyed as a machine. **EPH** is a hated binge with a real rush — high compulsiveness, mid retrospective enjoyment. **3,4-CTMP** stimulates hard and scores low enjoyment. Hated binge ≠ low euphoria; CTMP is the exception that really is both too much *and* not fun.

## Pharmacology in one board (not a dose table)

Amphetamines are mostly **releasers**. Phenidates are **DAT/NET blockers** (Luethi 2017: no efflux, cocaine-shaped mechanism). That is why EPH can feel coke-adjacent and 2-FMA still feels like speed’s cousin.

Fluoro position rule from forum consensus, not theory: **2- = work**, **3- = more fun / more messy**, **4- = serotonin + stim**. N-methyl usually lengthens and dirties.

Phenidate transporter vs feel (Luethi / Brandt lab, HEK IC50 / Ki — relative, not a how-to):

| SKU | DAT/NET paper story | Functional feel |
|---|---|---|
| **3,4-CTMP** | Most potent DAT/NET on the shelf. Far above MPH. | Too much. Anxiety, vasoconstriction, 5-HT2B valve rumor. |
| **4F-MPH** | MPH-class or a bit stronger. Clean DAT/NET, no SERT. | Focus monster. The one people actually reordered. |
| **4-Me-TMP** | MPH-like DAT/NET. | Milder analog. Functional. |
| **MPH** | The parent blocker. | Dry focus. Some mood if IR. |
| **EPH** | DAT still hot; NET weaker than MPH. | Short recreational. Fiend. Caustic. |
| **IPPH** | DAT similar to EPH, NET weaker, longer in rumor. | Study drug. Little rush. |
| **PPH** | Weaker than EPH/IPPH as chain grows. | Quiet stamp. |
| **HDEP-28** | Only phenidate with real SERT in that paper. | Mood-wander, often unpleasant / long. |
| **methylmorphenate / N-benzyl-EPH** | Weak. | Almost no human chorus. |

## 07 — Amphetamine / meth / fluoroamps / stim methoxyamps

### Amphetamine (speed, amph, benzedrine leftover, Adderall mix)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: Everything else is a remix.

#### The hit

- **Rush 48 / B** — Oral is a climb. Insufflated street speed has a snap that is real and still not meth. Old “speed freak” lore is route-gated; do not write an SOP.
- **Euphoria 68 / A** — Talky, confident, music-okay, grind-okay. Racemic paste is dirtier and more jitter than pharma dex. The mood is why people stay.
- **Enjoyment 74 / A** — The baseline night. Work or weekend. People come back.
- **Compulsiveness 58 / A** — Redose because it fades, not because it vacuums the room like a pyro. Binges happen. They are not flakka.

#### The bill

Jaw, wake, comedown gray, appetite gone. Street speed is a lottery (cut, 4-FA, 4-MA, caffeine). Heart tax is ordinary stimulant tax. Paranoia if you live there.

NPC line: “Everything else is a remix.”  
Sources: NIDA / DEA amph-class facts; Erowid amph vault; forum consensus vs meth.  
Gaps: enantiomer feel (dex vs levo) is pharma lore more than street SKU talk.

### 4-FA (4-fluoroamphetamine, Flux, 4-FMP, PAL-303)

Status: well_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: MDMA for people who have a shift.
