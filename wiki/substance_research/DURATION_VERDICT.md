# Duration verdict — MDPV sit-call (and supported pyro clocks)

Game catalog. **Not a cookbook.** No recipes. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. No injection SOP. Every integer below is a `gameplay_estimate`.

This file closes the ten `_dur_01`…`_dur_10` notes. It is the house sit, not a new research pass.

**Duration** = one-dose *pleasurable fun window* (minutes). Used only in `WeightedDuration` / catalogue Enjoyment.  
**HalfLife** = gameplay decay for `RemainingExposure` only. Not clinical t½. Not a dose guide.  
Binge nights, leftover wake, “still going at hour eight,” hospital 6–8 h, and 2–48 h after-effects live on **C / Aftereffects / HalfLife**. They are not Duration.

Designer lock: MDPV lasts **way longer** than α-PVP. Retracted **48** must not return. Any MDPV D ≤ locked α-PVP **90** is inverted.

## The 180 vs 240 fight

Sister notes agreed on **direction** (MDPV lasts way longer than locked 90) and fought the **integer**.

| Camp | D | Who | What they counted |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **180** | 180 | `_dur_03` head-to-head, `_dur_04` PW/Erowid, `_dur_07` reviews | Fun window = peak / plateau / desired-effect band. DEA 2–3 h, Horsley 120–210, Penders 2–4 h, Erowid nasal total 2–3.5 h, PW peak 1–4 h. Double locked 90. |
| **240** | 240 | `_dur_01` forums, `_dur_09` HalfLife pair, `_dur_10` binge-vs-dose | Bluelight “a line lasts 3–5 h,” oral 4–6 to baseline, Helsing / exp 94286 morning-ritual glow ~4 h. 2.67× the 90. Still under leftover 8 h. |
| **210** | **210** | `_dur_05` MD-family (this sit) | Midpoint of the **180–240 sit-band**. Neighbor on the stim board is NEP 200, not Dex 280 / meth 400. |

**210 is the sit.** It is not a claim that leftover eight hours is Duration. Hour eight (lip still going, heart, no sleep, Ross/DEA intoxication 6–8 h, Erowid after-effects 2–48 h) stays off D. `_dur_08` already forbade stealing the vape half-hour into the catalogue integer; `_dur_10` forbade stealing the binge night.

Floor if someone reopens the fight: **180** still beats the lock. Ceiling: **240**. Fail: ≤ 90 (including 48), ≥ 300 (eats leftover / meth).

HalfLife is a **different fight**. `_dur_01` wanted **220** (overnight wired). `_dur_09` wanted **160** (decay only; rejected 220 as aftereffects). House **180** sits between those two recommendations. It is not Anizan’s rat 78–98 min, not Maker B’s old 100, not equal to 210, and not the 8-hour sentence.

α-PVP stays **90 / 140**. `_dur_02` found no hard conflict. Forum vape bang is often 15–45; residual wake is HalfLife, not a reason to move 90.
