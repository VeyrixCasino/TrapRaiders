# Assignment 06 — Substituted / unusual pyrovalerones

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a manual.** No recipes. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. Scores are unitless **0–100 or null**. **Rush / euphoria / enjoyment / compulsiveness are independent.** A chemical can empty the bag and still be a bad night. Unknown = `null`, never `0`.

Family: ring-stamped and odd-ring **pyrrolidine cathinones** — methyl / fluoro / chloro / bromo / methoxy on the phenyl, PiHP position isomers, the 1960s parent **pyrovalerone**, and the dusty odd-ring shelf (naphthyl / thienyl / tetralin / indanyl). Wikipedia’s designer-drug pyro list was a **discovery seed only**. Every name below was checked against STRIDA, EMCDDA/EUDA formal notifications, Cayman-as-reference, and forensic IDs. Unsubstituted α-PXP and MD-pyros live in assignments 04 / 05. They appear here only as comparison notes.

**Out (owned elsewhere):** α-PPP, α-PBP, α-PVP, α-PHP, α-PiHP, α-PEP/PV-8, α-POP/PV-9, α-PCYP. MDPPP, MDPBP, MDPV, MDPHP, MDPiHP, MDPEP, MDPOP. 2,3-MDPV, 5-DBFPV (almost-MD, Kit H).

## How this shelf feels (one board)

Most pyros are DAT/NET blockers, not releasers. That is the fiend machine: coke-shaped wanting, no serotonin hug, redose until the lights look like police. **Naphyrone is the exception on paper** (SNDRI, cocaine-balanced). **TH-PVP is the exception in animals** (SERT-leaning, failed to stimulant-behave). **Pleasure and compulsiveness do not travel together.** 4F-α-PVP can be a better night than α-PVP and still steal the weekend. 4-Me-α-PiHP can be a DAT monster and still taste like a stamp.

| Slider | High | Mid | Low / empty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rush** | 4F-α-PVP (vape), 4-Me-α-PiHP | pyrovalerone, 4F-PHP, 4Cl-α-PVP | 4-MePPP, MOPPP, TH-PVP |
| **Euphoria** | naphyrone *if real*; some 4F-α-PVP nights | pyrovalerone, MPHP, 4-MeO-PVP | 4-MePPP, TH-PVP, 4F-PV8 |
| **Enjoyment** | 4F-α-PVP (split: better than PHP / still a pyro) | pyrovalerone, naphyrone-if-real | 4-MePPP, MOPPP, Me-PiHP tox cases |
| **Compulsiveness** | 4F-α-PVP, 4-Me-α-PiHP, 4Cl-α-PVP | MPHP, α-PVT, 4F-PHP | TH-PVP, MOPPP, 4-MePPP |
| **Identity risk** | **NRG-1 / naphyrone** | NRG-3 (MPPP mix), “4-Me-PVP” | German 4-Me wall (usually what it says) |

Comparison anchors (not this file’s SKUs): **α-PVP** is the flakka ceiling — social then sirens, compulsiveness near the top of the family. **MDPV** is the 2010 monster and the powder that was *inside* a lot of “naphyrone” bags. **α-PHP** is the later body-heavy vape favorite. Ring stamps and odd rings sit around those three, they do not replace them.

### German / Centroton wall (4-Me on the ring, walk the tail)

This is the *actual* parent series. Pyrovalerone was a 1960s fatigue pill (Centroton, Thymergix). West Germany then walked the tail as designer drugs: 4-MePPP (C3) → MPBP (C4) → pyrovalerone (C5) → MPHP (C6). Old catalogs listed them next to MDPPP. Milder than flakka. Medicine-cabinet lore, not zombie headlines.

#### Pyrovalerone — 4-Me-α-PVP / Centroton / Thymergix / O-2371

The polite ancestor. Schedule V leftover. Cayman 10836 exists because labs still need the name. Withdrawn after abuse, not after “bath salt” TV. Meltzer 2006 and Simmler 2013: potent DAT/NET blocker, weak SERT, not a releaser. Users who met both say it is **α-PVP’s grandfather who still has a job**. Compulsive enough to lose the prescription. Not compulsive enough to make the news.

### Fluoro / chloro / bromo stamps (the STRIDA sticker wall)

Beck et al. 2018, STRIDA 2011–2016: **114 intoxications, 11 copycats**. This file owns the ring-stamped ones. Top three overall were α-PBP, MDPHP (other assignments), and **4F-α-PVP**. Clinical picture: agitation, delirium, hallucinations, seizures, tachycardia, hypertension, hyperthermia. Poly-drug almost always. Only 8/114 were a single pyro.

#### 4F-α-PVP — 4-fluoro-PVP / pFPVP

The fluoro flakka. STRIDA’s most-seen *substituted* pyro. Cayman 15166. Japan 2014 market. Fatal poly-cathinone blood with 4-MeO-PVP + PV8 + 4-FMC. Forum split is the point: some nights **better than α-PHP** — coke-shaped, happy, “ridiculous urge to redose” even when the mood is good. Deep α-PVP binges grew sirens and dead relatives; 4F reports more often stay *level* and still empty the gram. Vape is the rumor ROA (`vape_fav`). Harsh. Compulsiveness is not a side effect. It is the product.

### PiHP methyl position isomers (Sweden 2020–2024)

Grumann / Swedish NFC 2025 (*Forensic Sci Int*): **113 seizures, 37 tox cases, 5 autopsies**. 4-Me first (2020, already scheduled 2019), then 2-Me (2022; 36 seizures in 2023), then 3-Me (2023, 26 seizures). So far **Sweden-only in the literature** — regional micro-market or under-reporting. DAT IC50: **4-Me 0.5 nM > 3-Me 1.7 nM > 2-Me 7.6 nM**. All three DAT/SERT **>40× cocaine**. Co-detected with amps, benzos, opioids. Cap the UI at these three plus the two fluoro-PiHPs. **No 3×4 isomer grid.**

#### 4-Me-α-PiHP

First on the board. Most tox detections (peak 20 forensic cases in 2021). Nastiest DAT number on this entire shelf. Regional, not global — and still five autopsies in one country.

### Odd-ring shelf (Kit J)

One dusty shelf. Not a third wall. Naphyrone is the only name NPCs remember — and they remember the **sticker**.

#### Naphyrone — β-naphyrone / O-2482 / naphthylpyrovalerone

Meltzer 2006: the **only** pyro analog in that set that is an SNDRI at nanomolar on all three transporters. Simmler 2013: cocaine-shaped inhibition profile; **not a releaser**; more SERT than MDPV or pyrovalerone, so the *theory* is more pleasure, less pure fiend. Derungs case and ACMD 2010 treated it as a real stim.

**The bag lied.** Brandt 2010: 13 UK NRG-1 buys after the mephedrone ban — **one** looked like naphyrone. Seventy percent of NRG-1/NRG-2 were banned cathinones wearing a new label (MDPV, butylone, flephedrone, mephedrone…). Wood / Dargan “Energy-1” case: advertised NPV, **powder and serum were butylone + MDPV**. ACPOS: Scottish NRG-1 tested as MDPV. Genuine β-naphyrone *did* exist (Swedish 10 g + 288 g EMCDDA notifications; some later test-purchases). Scores below are for **the molecule**. Street reviews of “NRG-1” are mostly **MDPV reviews**.

## Identity-uncertain names (print these on the *sticker*, not as extra molecules)

1. **NRG-1 / Energy-1 / “naphyrone”** — brand. Often MDPV ± butylone ± flephedrone ± caffeine. Sometimes real β-naphyrone. Sometimes α+β mix.
2. **NRG-2 / NRG-3** — same era, contents wander. One NRG-3 = 4-MePPP + pentylone.
3. **MPPP** — here = 4-MePPP. Elsewhere = desmethylprodine (opioid / MPTP story). **Hard collision.**
4. **4-Me-PVP / 4-Me-α-PVP** — pyrovalerone, or a vendor who means α-PVP, or a vendor who means 4-Me-PiHP.
5. **α-naphyrone vs β-naphyrone** — both sold as naphyrone. Only β has the Meltzer numbers.
6. **TH-PVP “cyclohexane-α-PVP”** — wrong caption. Tetralin.
7. **5-PPDI vs 5-BPDI** — C4 vs C6 indane. Easy to swap on a menu.
8. **4F-PHiP vs 4F-PiHP vs 4F-PHP** — isohexyl vs hexyl. Labs mix these the way they mix MDPHP/MDPiHP.
9. **3,4-DMPV** — dimethoxy or dimethyl. Pick a structure or skip.
10. **PV-4** — MPHP. Not PV-8. Not 4-MePPP.

## Gaps

- No head-to-head human study of 4F-α-PVP vs α-PVP vs 4-Me-α-PiHP. Fiend ranking is DAT + STRIDA + forum weather.
