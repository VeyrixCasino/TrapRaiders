# Assignments 11 + 12 — MDMA stack and benzofuran / benzodioxane / aminoindane cousins

Game catalog for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a manual.** No recipes. No precursors. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. Scores are unitless **0–100 or `null`**. Unknown is `null`, never `0`.

Kits **O** (MDMA stack) and **P** (Benzo Fury). Methylone / ethylone / eutylone live in **Kit F** (bk-MDMA cathinones) — do not steal those SKUs. PMA / PMMA / 4-MTA are methoxy / methylthio scare pills, not methylenedioxy relatives. 2C-B-FLY / bromo-DragonFLY are psychedelic benzodifurans (other assignment). **No opioids.**

Lead axes: **enjoyment, euphoria, openness / empathy, then rush if people describe a come-up hit.** Compulsiveness is scored independently — a soft roll can still steal the bag if the night is short. Extra feel when evidenced: **empathy, music, tactile.** Negatives below: afterglow crash, heart scare (especially 5-APB / 5-HT2B), temperature.

**Two voices, kept apart:**

- **Pharmacology / clinic** — transporter ratios, 5-HT2A/2B, controlled human studies.
- **Forum / street** — “it’s just like Molly” is a sales line. Bags stamped Benzo Fury were often mixed or wrong. Rank copies by what people actually wrote after they had both, not by a vendor blurb.

## Assignment 11 — MDMA / MDA / MDEA / MBDB and documented MD-amphetamine relatives

### MDMA (Molly, Mandy, 3,4-MDMA)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: The roll. Kitchen brick. Everything after this is a cover band.

#### The hit

- **Rush 60 / A** — Not a pyro snap. Users and older ecstasy writeups describe a short “coming on” then a plateau. Some feel a body-wave hit on the come-up; clinic is smoother than forum poetry.
- **Euphoria 78 / A** — Well-being, happiness, self-esteem. The number the copies chase.
- **Enjoyment bar 45 / enjoyment_x 2.5 (A)** — The night people compare every RC to. ×coke, not a 92-next-to-PVP.
- **Compulsiveness 38 / B** — Pill redose is real. DAT/SERT is serotonergic; abuse liability is lower than amphetamine / pyros. Do not score this like α-PVP.
- **Reinforcement 42 / A** — Low DAT vs SERT predicts less dependence than stims (Simmler / Liechti).
- **Empathy 95 / A** — Closeness, trust, reduced fear. Nichols’ entactogen. Hysek / Liechti lab nights. Mouse work later tied NAc 5-HT to empathy-like behavior — mechanism story, not a trip report.
- **Emotional openness 96 / A** — Authenticity, lowered social threat, saying the thing.
- **Sociability 94 / A** — Liking company. The rave and the living-room both work.
- **Music 90 / A** — Rave staple. Not subtle.
- **Tactile 88 / A** — Skin, hugs, “everything feels expensive.”

#### The bill

- **Afterglow crash 70 / A** — Weekend high → midweek low is in the papers (Curran 1997; Verheyden gender split). Clinic nights can leave an afterglow instead — sleep / setting confound. Street still writes Suicide Tuesday.
- **Cardiovascular 65 / A** — HR, BP, NE release.
- **Temperature 72 / A** — The hyperthermia everyone already knows.
- **Heart scare 40 / B** — Setola et al.: MDMA/MDA can poke 5-HT2B / valve cells *in vitro*. Acute street scare is heat and heart-rate, not fen-phen headlines.
- **Sleep 70 / A** — The night does not end when the club does.
- **Paranoia 25 / B** — Not the main plot unless overheat / bad pill / comedown.

NPC line: “The roll. Kitchen brick.”  
Flags: `oral_ok` `mislabel` (pills)  
Gaps: Street “Molly” identity. Enantiomer talk is real in papers, not a second SKU.

## Assignment 12 — Benzofuran / benzodioxane / aminoindane

### 5-MAPB (N-methyl-5-APB)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: Closest thing to MDMA after the drought. This is the one they meant.

#### The hit

- **Rush 62 / B** — Forum majority: come-up is the MDMA one — heart, stim, then a dopamine-ish wave — then it melts into the serotonin sofa. That come-up hit is why rush scores above 6-APB.
- **Euphoria 86 / B** — Repeated “closest RC” threads (Bluelight 5-MAPB vs 6-APB; r/researchchemicals comparisons). Still “lacks the profoundness” of first-time MDMA.
- **Enjoyment 88 / B** — Gentler than MDA-edge 6-APB for people who wanted a hug, not a vision.
- **Compulsiveness 45 / B** — Shorter than 6-APB → more same-night redose, MDMA-shaped. Not a pyro.
- **Empathy 86 / B** — The copy’s selling point.
- **Emotional openness 88 / B** — Closest drought-era chest-open.
- **Sociability 84 / B** — Talk and sofa, not just dance.
- **Music 80 / B**
- **Tactile 82 / B** — Skin/sofa reports more than 6-APB dance reports.
- **Pharmacology:** Brandt 2020 — 5-MAPB is a triple releaser, *more potent on the dish than MDMA*. Sahai / Davidson: DAT slowdown / reverse in accumbens tissue. Shimshoni: 5-HT2 family binding. **5-MAPB metabolizes to 5-APB** — the heart card rides along. Rickli 2015 did **not** include 5-MAPB; do not fake a DAT/SERT from that table.

#### The bill

- **Afterglow crash 48 / B** — Split: many “almost no Tuesday” until they used it like candy. Abuse brings the crash back.
- **Cardiovascular 70 / B** — Hofer 2017 confirmed oral 5-MAPB: pale/clammy, hypertension, troponin, ECG change, mild hyperthermia, clonus, agitation. Vasoconstrictor read.
- **Temperature 60 / B** — Fuwa rat work: 5-HT + temp + lethality; they pointed at the 5-APB metabolite.
- **Heart scare 72 / B** — Acute case + 5-HT2B family + metabolite is 5-APB.
- **Sleep 62 / C**
- **Paranoia 32 / C** — Less than 5+6 combo nights.

NPC line: “This is the one they meant.”  
Flags: `oral_ok`  
Evidence: high forum, medium tox case, good animal/in-vitro. No MDMA-vs-5-MAPB RCT.

### 6-APB (Benzo Fury)

Status: moderately_documented  
Market: rc_era / street legal-high  
Headline: All-night Benzo Fury. Slow come-up. Dance until the lights come on.

#### The hit

- **Rush 48 / B** — I-TREND Dutch forums (1022 posts): shivers at 30–60 min, then **slow waves**, uneasy/awkward come-up, full euphoria another hour later. Not a snap. Some Bluelight: barely empathic until the night is already long.
- **Euphoria 80 / B** — “First-time ecstasy” language shows up. Same threads: slightly less euphoric than E, clearer head, more energy, time slower.
- **Enjoyment 82 / B** — One pellet / one night. The duration *is* the product.
- **Compulsiveness 32 / B** — The night is already 6–8 h (peak 3–4). Redose stretches it into “cannot sleep.” Less fiend than a short roll.
- **Empathy 70 / B** — More MDA than MDMA in the majority compare. Hug shows up later, or when they sit down.
- **Emotional openness 72 / B** — Later in the night, if at all.
- **Sociability 76 / B** — Club drug. Dance together more than confess.
- **Music 85 / B** — Club/festival drug. “Waves when you dance, subtle when you sit” (I-TREND).
- **Tactile 65 / B** — Present; dance > skin vs 5-MAPB.
- **Pharmacology:** Rickli DAT/SERT ~0.29 (more DA relative than 5-APB). Potent 5-HT2B agonist (Iversen / ACMD). Brandt: more potent releaser than MDA. Monte 1993 was **APDB**, not this.

#### The bill

- **Afterglow crash 58 / B** — Split file: I-TREND headaches / empty / days-after hangover **stronger than MDMA** for some; Bluelight “no suicide Tuesday” for others. Score the headache file; do not erase the split.
- **Cardiovascular 72 / B** — Tachycardia, palpitations, UK deaths in the Benzo Fury years (bags not always 6-APB).
- **Temperature 68 / B** — Feverish after, sympathomimetic case series.
- **Heart scare 75 / A** — 5-HT2B full agonist story is the ACMD’s long-term valve warning. Acute: ischemia / vasoconstriction talk in ED reviews.
- **Sleep 78 / B** — After a redose, sleep is a rumor.
- **Paranoia 40 / C** — End-of-night hallucinations in some I-TREND notes; 5+6 combo was nastier (nystagmus, paranoid thoughts).

**Benzo Fury is a sticker.** I-TREND shop buys: 6-APB labeled 5-APB, 5+6 mixes, one “6-APB” that was **MDPV**, one “5-EAPB” that was 5-APB. 4-APB is a known by-product in 6-APB goods (Stanczuk / Strano Rossi).

NPC line: “All-night. The name on the pellet.”  
Flags: `oral_ok` `mislabel` `OD`  
Evidence: fat forum + I-TREND + ACMD + Rickli. No RCT vs MDMA.
