# Assignment 03 — Methylenedioxy cathinones (bk-MDMA family)

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a manual.** No recipes. No precursors. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. No injection SOP. Scores are unitless **0–100 or null** inferences from public reports, papers, and forensic lists. **Enjoyment / euphoria / rush / compulsiveness are scored independently** — a chemical can hug like Tuesday-night Molly and still empty the bag, or feel like cold speed and still get pressed as ecstasy.

Family: **β-keto methylenedioxyamphetamines** — the “-ylone” wall. Same MD stamp as MDMA, cathinone backbone. Kit F. Share art with pressed pills + crystals.

**Out (owned elsewhere):** MD-pyros (MDPV, MDPPP, MDPBP, MDPHP, MDPiHP, MDPEP, 2,3-MDPV) → assignment 05. Methedrone / bk-PMMA is 4-MeO, not MD → assignment 01. 3,4-DMMC is **dimethyl**, not methylenedioxy — EUDA’s old interactive table printed the wrong R3 on that row; do not ship it here. N-ethylhexedrone (hexen) is **not** MD.

## Scope

**In (full cards):** methylone, ethylone, butylone, pentylone, eutylone, ephylone (N-ethylpentylone), dipentylone (N,N-dimethylpentylone), dimethylone, dibutylone.

**In (documented relatives, thinner):** MDC / bk-MDA; BMDP / benzylone; BMDB; N-propylbutylone (bk-PBDB / putylone); N-isopropylbutylone; N-cyclohexyl methylone; N-cyclohexyl butylone; N-butylpentylone; N-ethylhexylone; N,N-diethylpentylone; diethylone; hexylone; tertylone; N-ethyl-N-methylbutylone.

**In (positional isomers, lab / forensic):** 2,3-methylone, 2,3-ethylone, 2,3-butylone. Synthesized so labs can tell them from the 3,4 street isomers. Seized bags in the 2011 Dublin paper were **3,4**.

## How this shelf feels (one board)

The MD stamp is a **promise**, not a personality. Short-chain N-methyl (methylone) still hugs. Lengthen the tail or the N and the hug dies; DAT wins; they press it as Molly anyway.

| Slider | Molly-shaped | Hybrid / fake roll | Cold stim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enjoyment** | **methylone** | ethylone, dibutylone (split), butylone | eutylone, pentylone, ephylone, dipentylone |
| **Euphoria** | methylone | ethylone (subtle waves), butylone (stim-euphoria) | ephylone, dipentylone, N-cyclohexyl methylone |
| **Rush** | pentylone (route-myth: some ROAs hit hard) | methylone (fast, short), eutylone vape talk | ephylone (sharp, not satisfying) |
| **Compulsiveness** | ephylone, dipentylone, pentylone, eutylone | methylone (chase the short night) | BMDP (nothing to chase) |
| **Sociability / hug** | **methylone**, ethylone | butylone, dibutylone (split reports) | **ephylone, dipentylone, eutylone high-dose** |
| **Pressed as Molly** | all of them after ~2014 | eutylone 2019–21 king | dipentylone 2021–24 king |

Pharmacology that backs the board (not a dose chart): methylone is an SNDRA, weaker than MDMA, faster on / faster off in the only human RCT. Lengthen α-carbon (butylone, pentylone, eutylone) and you get **hybrid** drugs — DAT blockers that still dump 5-HT. Pentylone is the most DAT-leaning of that trio and the strongest rat self-admin. Dipentylone: DAT IC50 49 nM, SERT 4990 nM — a hundred-fold cold. That is not a roll. That is a pressed lie.

## Agent cards

Scores: 0–100 or `null`. Pleasure ≠ compulsiveness. Confidence A/B/C/D/U.

### Methylone — bk-MDMA / M1 / Explosion

Tuesday-night Molly. The only one on this wall with a **controlled human comparison to MDMA** (Poyatos 2023): stimulation, euphoria, wellbeing, empathy, faster onset, earlier disappearance. Shulgin already called it — almost the potency, **not the unique magic**. Erowid positives read like a roll: mood lift, love, closeness, music, “this moment is perfect.” Bluelight: hour-and-a-half to two hours of *kinda* rolling, jaw and eye-wiggles, then **poof** — so people redose chasing a peak that never quite arrives. Explosion bags (NL ~2004) and moonrock “molly” are the origin myth.

Rush is a lift, not a slam poem. Enjoyment stays high because the hug is real. Compulsiveness is the **short clock**, not pyro vacuum.

### Eutylone — bk-EBDB / N-ethylbutylone

They pressed it in Molly pills anyway. 2019–21 US king after ephylone got heat; CDC 2020 death note; WHO 2021 scheduled it. Rush is mid. Enjoyment is mid-low. Compulsiveness is high because the first hour is “is this it?” and the bag answers.

### Dipentylone — N,N-dimethylpentylone / DMP / bk-DMBDP

Counterfeit Ecstasy/Molly. 125-case forensic series (2025): blood all over the map; pentylone in 98% as the N-desmethyl metabolite. DAT 49 nM vs SERT 4990 nM. Locomotor cousin of eutylone / NEP / pentylone. Human **feel writing is thin** — the market did not wait for poems. Infer cold stim from the transporter ratio + the Molly-press pattern, not from a vault that does not exist.

Labs mix it with N-ethylpentylone, N-isopropylbutylone, hexylone, tertylone, diethylone, N-propylbutylone, N-ethyl-N-methylbutylone. Same formula neighborhood. **Separate SKUs or the lab lies.**

## Molly vs cold stim (the actual question)

**Feel like Molly (empathogenic, with a stim tax):**

1. **Methylone** — only honest “MDMA lite.” Hug is documented, not folklore.
2. **Ethylone** — same family, quieter, easier to miss.
3. **Dibutylone** — *if* you believe the warm-sparkle camp. Other camp says junk. Split.
4. **Butylone** — MD shirt, stim body. Partial Molly.

**Hybrid / fake roll (pressed as Molly, body is stim):**

5. **Eutylone** — the 2019–21 festival liar.
6. **Pentylone** — first lone that self-admins like it means it.
7. **Dimethylone** — rats vote MDMA; humans didn’t file a report.

**Cold stim (DAT story, hug optional, deaths not optional):**

8. **Ephylone** — news, heart, paranoia, weak empathy.
9. **Dipentylone** — 100× DAT over SERT. Current-wave Molly stamp.
10. **N-cyclohexyl methylone** — ecstasy tablet, convulsant in the mouse.
11. **N-cyclohexyl butylone** — meth/coke in the box, not MDMA.
12. **N-isopropylbutylone** — everywhere in 2024–25 labs; feel unknown, treat as cold until a thread exists.
13. **BMDP** — cold *and* empty.

**Not a product:** MDC, 2,3-isomers, hexylone/tertylone/diethylone-as-SKU.

- Dipentylone pleasure scores are **inferred from DAT/SERT**, confidence C. Need actual reports before you ship a confident enjoyment number.
- N-isopropylbutylone is a market headliner in 2024–25 NFLIS and still has **zero usable feel**.
- “Cyputylone” will print on vendor art; CFSRE says don’t. Use N-cyclohexyl methylone.
- Ephylone abbreviation **NEP** collides with N-ethylpentedrone (Kit E). Always disambiguate.
- Pentylone in a tox screen after 2021 often means **dipentylone parent**. Don’t double-count SKUs in a raid.
- 2,3-isomers: reference standards, not bags. Don’t add a 2,3 wall.
- MDC stability (dimer) is chemistry lore; we did not find a modern street seizure series.
- Hexylone / tertylone / diethylone / N-ethyl-N-methylbutylone: named for chromatography. Product status unproven in the pages we opened.
- N-ethylhexylone vs N-ethylhexedrone will get mixed in chatter. Only the first is this kit.
- 5-methyl-ethylone remains **unverified_claim**.
- Route myths (vape / slam) exist for pentylone and ephylone. Catalog flags only. No SOP.
- Sociability for later-wave lones is mostly “sold as Molly, felt like speed” — not a sociability study.
