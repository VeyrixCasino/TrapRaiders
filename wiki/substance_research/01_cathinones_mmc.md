# Assignment 01 — Methyl / halogen / methoxy cathinones

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a cookbook.**

- No recipes, precursors, synthesis, glassware, yields.
- No doses-as-how-to. No injection SOP. Route notes are flags (`slam_only`, `vape_fav`, `oral_ok`, `redose`, `mislabel`).
- No opioids. No invented names. Unknown scores are **null**, never 0.
- Writeup tone leads with the hit. Compulsiveness is scored independently of pleasure: “hated it, stayed up a week, threw it away” is high compulsiveness, not low euphoria.
- Status is about **bags on tables**, not chemical possibility.

**Scope:** ring-methyl / halogen / methoxy methcathinones, plus the MEC / EEC / CEC / EMC wall, methedrone, mexedrone, DMMC isomers, and other documented names that actually appear in EWS, WHO, forensic, or old forum catalogs. Chain-length drones (buphedrone, pentedrone, NEP, hexen) and methylenedioxy / pyro kits are **out** — later assignments.

**Isomer rule:** 2- / 3- / 4- are different SKUs. Same formula, different night. Labs that only run GC-MS without IR/NMR will lie to you. `MCAT` is a street word that has meant **methcathinone** and **mephedrone**. **Methedrone ≠ mexedrone ≠ mephedrone ≠ methylone.** **4-EMC ≠ 4-MEC.** **DMMC** on a bag almost always means **3,4-DMMC**, not N,N-dimethylcathinone.

Confidence: **A** many independent user reports + official; **B** several forum/official strands; **C** thin; **D** analog inference / single strand; **U** no feel data.

## Kit A — MMC (methyl-methcathinone)

### 4-MMC (mephedrone)

**Status:** `market_confirmed`  
**Aliases:** mephedrone, 4-methylmethcathinone, meow, meow meow, MCAT (ambiguous), drone, 4-MMC  
**One-liner:** The one. Everything after this is a cover band.

**RUSH.** Snorted: the hit old UK threads treated like a religious event — coke-shaped, immediate, “pleasurable rushing” on the Erowid card. Oral: slower, still a lift that people compared to MDMA-plus-speed without the visuals.

**EUPHORIA.** The legend. Empathy, mood lift, music, talk. Pre-ban batches are still the ghost every analog is measured against. Some later heads swear modern 4-MMC is weaker; the *shape* of the night is the same.

**COMPULSIVENESS.** Extreme. Erowid’s first negative is “strong desire to redose, craving to recapture initial euphoric rush.” Bags evaporate. “We simply kept going until there was nothing left.” High pleasure and high fiend live in the same molecule.

**ENJOYMENT.** Highest in the family when the stamp is real. Short night, huge smile, social engine.

**NEGATIVES.** Jaw, sweat, palpitations, insomnia, memory holes, a comedown that scales with how hard you chased the first rush. Insufflation wrecks the nose. Not physically “dope-sick,” psychologically greedy.

### 3-MMC (metaphedrone)

**Status:** `market_confirmed`  
**Aliases:** 3-methylmethcathinone, metaphedrone, 3-MMC  
**One-liner:** Diet 4-MMC. Fine until the bag is NEP.

**RUSH.** Real, shorter-hug than 4-MMC for most. Some crystal batches made old heads swear they could not blind it. Most later bags: more speed, less velvet.

**EUPHORIA.** The stand-in that actually worked. Less serotonin hug, more grind + talk. Chemsex favorite. Batch lottery is the whole story — “incredible” and “underwhelming but I did a gram anyway” sit in the same threads.

**COMPULSIVENESS.** High even when they call it meh. “I hated it, stayed up, finished the bag” is the 3-MMC review. EUDA drug-checking: samples sold as 3-MMC often **are** 2-MMC. If it lasts till noon, it was not 3-MMC.

**ENJOYMENT.** Best of the copies when genuine. Not the poem. Still a night.

**NEGATIVES.** Tachycardia, binge, chemsex harm cluster, withdrawal after daily use (WHO). After 2022 control, the name stayed on bags that were not 3-MMC.

## Kit B — CMC (chloro)

### 4-CMC (clephedrone)

**Status:** `market_confirmed`  
**Aliases:** clephedrone, 4-chloromethcathinone, 4-CMC  
**One-liner:** Same night, worse morning.

**RUSH.** Weaker onset than 3-MMC in the 2014 Bluelight binge writeups; still a night. WHO: concentration, confidence, sociability.

**EUPHORIA.** Serotonin-leaning stim, MDMA-shaped at a discount. Human oral-fluid study and forum both call it empathogen-stim, not flakka.

**COMPULSIVENESS.** High. “Redosed and redosed… BAM.” Neurotoxin rumor (4-CA / 4-CMA cousin story) did **not** stop the EU seizure table — 4-CMC was #2 by count in 2022, still a 2024 tonne SKU.

**ENJOYMENT.** Cheap meow. Fun until the chlorine tax.

**NEGATIVES.** Nose burn, ugly comedowns, agitation, psychosis/hallucination cluster in WHO, sleeplessness, fatal and non-fatal intoxications (usually polydrug). The “never touch chlorine” sermon is loud; the market ignored it.

## Kit E — MEC / EEC / CEC / EMC

N-ethyl and ring-ethyl stamps on the same meow wall. **4-MEC ≠ 4-EMC.** MEC = 4-methyl + N-ethyl. EMC = 4-ethyl + N-methyl.

1. **2-CMC / 2-BMC / 2-FMC nights** — isomer analytics exist; user reports under *confirmed* ortho stamps do not.
