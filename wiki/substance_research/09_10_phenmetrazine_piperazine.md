# Assignments 09 + 10 — Phenmetrazines, aminorex / oxazolines, piperazines

Game catalog for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a manual.** No recipes. No precursors. No how-to-take. No milligram instructions. Scores are unitless **0–100 or unknown**. Pleasure, compulsiveness, and later misery are independent — a hated binge can still score high compulsiveness.

These are **four chemically different walls** that sat on the same RC / legal-high tables. Do not collapse them.

| Wall | Scaffold | Kit / rack |
|---|---|---|
| Phenmetrazines | phenylmorpholine | Kit U crystal bag |
| Aminorex | 2-amino-5-aryloxazoline | dusty RC / 80s underground |
| Oxazolinones | pemoline-class 4-oxazolidinones | nootropic crate next to 3-FPM |
| Piperazines | benzyl- vs phenyl-piperazine | NZ party-pill era, then ecstasy adulterant |

Neighbor pipradrols (2-DPMP / D2PM) sat on the same 2010s legal-high tables. They are **not** phenmetrazines. Short cards only.

## 09a — Phenmetrazines (phenylmorpholines)

Parent: **phenmetrazine** (Preludin, “prellies”). DAT/NET releaser, weak SERT. Same mesh as Kit U. Stamp = ring halogen / methyl / chain.

UNODC has logged a baker’s dozen novel phenmetrazines; this file only names ones with a paper, a seizure, or a real bag. Do not invent 2-/3-/4- grids beyond what circulated.

### Phenmetrazine (Preludin, prellies, Fenmetrazine)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: The old heads’ amphetamine. Diet-pill that escaped the bottle.

#### The hit
- **Rush** 62 / B — oral is a climb; old street myth says the other routes were the poem. Do not write the poem as a tutorial.
- **Euphoria** 72 / B — amphetamine-shaped lift, talk, appetite gone. Jazz / 60s–70s “prellies” lore is real affection, not RC coping.
- **Enjoyment** 78 / B — people sought it after the pharmacy stopped. That is love.
- **Compulsiveness** 80 / B — withdrawn as an anorectic *because* people would not leave it alone.

#### The bill
Paranoia mid-high on a binge. Insomnia. Cardio like any catecholamine releaser. Historical dependence is the reason the medicine died.

NPC line: “Prellies. Before the copies learned to lie.”

Sources:
- Rothman et al. 2002, *J Pharmacol Exp Ther* — DAT/NET releaser (paper)
- McLaughlin / Brandt 2018, *Drug Test Anal* — parent vs NPS analogs (paper)
- WHO 3-FPM critical review 2020 — Preludin withdrawn for abuse (review)
- Street name “prellies” in forensic/NPS intros (forensic)

Gaps: modern first-person file is thin; most love is historical. Do not invent a 2020s revival.

### 3-FPM (3-fluorophenmetrazine, 3-FPH, PAL-593)

Status: well_documented  
Market: rc_era  
Headline: Clean-functional liar. Still ruins weekends.

#### The hit
- **Rush** 48 / B — oral is a work drug. Insufflation burns (WHO / forum consensus) and is not the romance. Other routes get mentioned as the *actual* snap — flag only, not a lesson.
- **Euphoria** 52 / B — first hour: lift, talk, a little music, sometimes a fake empathy. Reddit: euphoria is short; redose can tease it back and then you live in the stimulation.
- **Enjoyment** 58 / B — people *bought kilos of this idea*. “I can file taxes / clean the flat / talk.” That is why the liar works.
- **Compulsiveness** 78 / A — Adamowicz: short action → repetition over hours or days, tolerance fast. WHO: habit-forming, psychological dependence talk. Kit U already had this: not as innocent as the reviews.

#### The bill
Anxiety, sweat, jaw, comedown 9–72 h (anxiety / fatigue / depression / irritability) lasting toward a week in the Polish writeup. STRIDA: tachycardia, agitation, delirium — always polydrug. Case reports of ugly IV outcomes (limb ischemia, livedo) — **slam myth is a fail-state, not a how-to.** Vein / weekend wrecker is the NPC rumor.

NPC line: “Clean functional. Ask the weekend.”

Sources:
- WHO ECDD 43rd, 3-FPM critical review, 2020 (review)
- Mayer et al. 2018, *Neuropharmacology* — 2/3/4-FPM DAT/NET releasers (paper)
- McLaughlin et al. 2017, *Drug Test Anal* — vendor 3-FPM vs isomers (forensic)
- Adamowicz & Gieroń 2016, *Probl Forensic Sci* — Polish market + user-effect summary (forensic)
- Bäckberg et al. 2016, STRIDA, *Clin Toxicol* (forensic)
- Bluelight EADD 3-FPM megathread (forum, qualitative only)
- r/researchchemicals redose-euphoria thread (forum)

Gaps: no controlled human study. Polydrug confounds STRIDA. Do not treat PAL numbers as street IDs.

## 09b — Aminorex family (2-amino-5-aryloxazolines)

Different ring from phenmetrazine. **Do not stamp these as Kit U.** Pulmonary-hypertension ghost follows the parent. 4-MAR is the only name on this wall people still get wet-eyed about.

### Aminorex (Menocil, Apiquel)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: Diet pill that closed a country. Recreational love never showed up.

#### The hit
- **Rush** unknown / U — stimulant / anorectic, not a 4-MAR memoir.
- **Euphoria** 28 / D — pharmacology says monoamine release. Street poems: almost none. Mostly a *medicine* and later a **levamisole → cocaine adulterant metabolite**.
- **Enjoyment** 22 / D — withdrawn 1968 after a pulmonary-hypertension epidemic (Switzerland/Austria/Germany). That is the story.
- **Compulsiveness** unknown / U

Once misrepresented as 4-MAR to dodge a law (Brewster & Davis 1991). That is a `mislabel` flag, not fandom.

#### The bill
Pulmonary arterial hypertension in susceptible people is the historical fail-state. Cardio. As a cocaine-cut metabolite the dose in users is usually tiny (Hess et al.) — do not pretend street coke is a secret aminorex roll.

NPC line: “The diet pill that drowned lungs. Nobody asked for an encore.”

Sources:
- Maier et al. 2018, *ACS Chem Neurosci* — aminorex analogs review (paper)
- Historical Menocil / Gurtner pulmonary hypertension literature (paper)
- Barker / Bertol / Ho — levamisole → aminorex (paper)
- Brewster & Davis 1991 — sold as 4-MAR (forensic)

Gaps: almost no modern recreational report corpus. Scores stay humble.

### 4-MAR (4-methylaminorex, U4Euh, U4Euh, Euphoria, 4-MAX, “Ice”)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: They named it Euphoria. For once the street name was not lying.

#### The hit
- **Rush** 58 / B — long climb, not a pyro snap. Smoked/insufflated lore exists; duration is the point.
- **Euphoria** 86 / A — Erowid titles are love letters: “Euphoric & Efficient,” “Real Ice,” “Clarity for Too Long.” Methamphetamine-shaped lift plus a clean, long plateau people still brag about decades later.
- **Enjoyment** 88 / A — **this is the human-love SKU on the aminorex wall.** Late-80s underground, then emergency control. Young RC users still ask for a revival they will not get.
- **Compulsiveness** 70 / B — you do not redose like mephedrone because the night does not end. People still chased the bag. Family pulmonary-hypertension case in manufacturers/users.

cis/trans matter in the lab (trans-(4S,5S) often hotter in animals). Street “Ice” also means meth — `mislabel` / identity fight.

#### The bill
A whole day, sometimes bleeding into the next (anecdote, not a stopwatch). Insomnia, cardio, rare pulmonary-hypertension signal, one published fatality with heart/lung failure. Do not treat “nootropic” claims as science.

NPC line: “U4Euh. The one the forum still lights a candle for.”

Sources:
- Erowid 4-Methylaminorex experience index (forum)
- Wikipedia / PsychonautWiki duration + effect summaries (secondary)
- Maier et al. 2018 (paper)
- Glennon / Batsche / historical scheduling (paper)
- Davis & Brewster fatality; pulmonary hypertension family case (paper)

Gaps: cis vs trans street identity often unknown. “Ice” name collides with meth.

## 10 — Piperazines

Two columns. **Benzyl = stim.** **Phenyl = serotonin-nasty** (nausea, anxiety, fake-roll). Historical product was usually a **blend**, not a molecule. NZ 2000s party pills sold tens of millions of units before 2008 Class C.

### BZP (1-benzylpiperazine, A2)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: Legal-high era. It was legal. That was the magic.

#### The hit
- **Rush** 45 / B — oral amphetamine-lite. About a tenth of amphetamine in old potency talk — feel is “dirty coffee with ambition,” not pyro.
- **Euphoria** 55 / B — real lift, talk, party energy. WHO: stimulant including euphoria. NZ youth actually used it (surveys ~40% of 18–29s in the peak years).
- **Enjoyment** 52 / B — **era love**, not molecule love. When MDMA was scarce / illegal and this was at the dairy, people liked the night. Modern heads call it harsh.
- **Compulsiveness** 48 / B — people stacked pills. Not 3-FPM-level fiend. Animal data: amphetamine-like.

Alone it is a **stim**. The “legal ecstasy” myth needs TFMPP in the same blister.

#### The bill
Jaw, insomnia, hangover, tachycardia, agitation, seizures in hospital series (usually mixes). Two-day crash shows up in user lore.

NPC line: “Party pill. The law was the high.”

Sources:
- WHO ECDD piperazine pre-reviews (review)
- NZ Expert Advisory Committee / 2008 classification debate (gov)
- Wilkins et al. NZ party-pill surveys (paper)
- UNODC ST/NAR/47 piperazines in seized materials (forensic)
- Erowid BZP vault (forum)

Gaps: many “BZP” nights were blends. Solo-BZP feel is cleaner in the lab than on the street.

- **Euphoria** 35 / B — 5-HT-heavy, mild hallucinogenic / fake-roll. Alone: often dysphoric.

- **Compulsiveness** 18 / B — you do not chase TFMPP. You chase the **mix**.

Serotonin-nasty column. The blender’s MDMA impression when stacked with BZP (~2:1 BZP:TFMPP in seized lore).

#### The bill
Nausea is the brand. Anxiety, headache. Hospital admissions were mixes.

NPC line: “That’s the twist they added so you’d think it was a roll.”

Sources: WHO ECDD TFMPP; NZ / EU party-pill analyses; CAHMA piperazine brief; Erowid.

### mCPP (1-(3-chlorophenyl)piperazine)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: The hangover in a pill.

#### The hit
- **Rush** 8 / A — there is no rush. There is a flu.
- **Euphoria** 12 / A — papers sometimes say “stimulant including euphoria.” Users say anxiety, panic, headache, nausea. Believe the users.
- **Enjoyment** 10 / A — **no human love.** Adulterant / fake-ecstasy. EU seized huge tablet counts (~823k tablets mentioned for 2008 in one methods paper).
- **Compulsiveness** 8 / A — 5-HT2C-shaped misery. Who redoses a hangover?

Metabolite of trazodone / nefazodone — that is pharmacy, not a party origin story.

#### The bill
Anxiety / panic (most frequent), nausea, headache, hallucinations. The experience *is* the aftereffect. `mislabel` when stamped as Molly.

NPC line: “If the pill feels like tomorrow morning, it was mCPP.”

Sources:
- WHO ECDD mCPP pre-review (review)
- EU tablet-seizure literature (forensic)
- Erowid mCPP experiences (forum)
- Trazodone metabolite pharmacology (paper)

Gaps: a minority of old reports claim a warm body. Do not let that overwrite the hangover consensus.

## Which of these had real human love vs just existed

**Real love (people asked for the name):**
- **4-MAR** — the only aminorex people still eulogize. Street names were compliments.
- **Phenmetrazine / prellies** — historical, genuine. Medicine that became a treat.
- **3-FPM** — love of the *idea* (clean functional). The molecule is a liar, but wallets opened. That counts as RC-era affection, not 4-MAR religion.
- **BZP+TFMPP packets** — NZ 2000s cultural moment. Love of legality + a night out. Not love of TFMPP.

**Era-tolerated / “it worked”:**
- **BZP alone** — stim that did a job.
- **Phendimetrazine** — script diversion, not poetry.
- **NMC / cyclazodone** — nootropic niche, not a dancefloor.

**Existed, infamous, or hated:**
- **mCPP** — hangover in a pill. No love.
- **TFMPP alone** — blender chemical. No love.
- **4,4′-DMAR** — brief curiosity, then a body count. Curiosity ≠ love.
- **Aminorex** — public-health villain / coke metabolite. Not a recreational sweetheart.
- **2-DPMP** — remembered the way a car crash is remembered.
- **MeOPP, MBZP, MDBZP, pFPP, DBZP** — packet filler.
- **4-FPM, 2-FPM, CPM isomers, MPM isomers, MDMAR, 4′F-4-MAR** — mostly existed on paper or in a customs bag.
- **Pemoline / fenozolone / thozalinone** — clinics and doping control, not Port Rook nightlife.

If the Flask door only gets three bottles from this whole document: **3-FPM**, **4-MAR** (dusty legend), **BZP+TFMPP party-pill tin**. Everything else is a stamp or a cautionary crate.
