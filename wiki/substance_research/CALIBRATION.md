# Stim / cath / pyro ruler (coke = 10)

Game catalog. **Not a cook book.**

**This ruler is stims, cathinones, and pyros only.** Dissos, psychedelics, benzos, MDMA-rolls, cannabinoids, deliriants are **not on this ladder**. Do not grade Dex against MXE.

Cocaine powder = **10 / 10 / 10 / 10** (rush / euphoria / enjoyment / compulsiveness). That 10 is a named, decent stim. Uncapped — no 100 ceiling.

Axes are independent. **Euphoria ≠ enjoyment ≠ compulsiveness ≠ rush.** A hated binge can have high compulsive + mid/high euphoria + low buy-again.

**Do not linear-map leftover 0–100.** Dexedrine 70 is not 70÷4 ≈ 18. That is how Vyvanse got a crazy 18. Re-seat every SKU by **comparison class**, then pick the number from head-to-heads.

Unknown = `null`, not `0`. No recipes, doses, ROA how-to. Route as a flag only.

## Reliability method (grade better)

1. Pick 2–3 Reddit / Bluelight / Erowid / Psychonaut head-to-heads per SKU vs **coke**, **4-MMC**, or **Dex / Vyvanse**. Cite them on the feel card.
2. **Band first, then number.** Never invent a 40/70/76 leftover.
3. **Sanity gate before write** — FAIL and fix if any of these fire:
   - `euphoria(homework) >= 12`
   - `euphoria(Dex) >= 16`
   - `euphoria(4-MMC) < 28`
   - `euphoria(Vyvanse) > 8`
4. `enjoyment` = buy-again. Do not “fix” a hated pyro into MDMA-cozy.
5. Unknown = `null`.
6. Dual boards: patch **both**. Do not clobber one into the other. Remaining fights stay flagged in README (4-MAR compulsive is one — do not fake-average).
