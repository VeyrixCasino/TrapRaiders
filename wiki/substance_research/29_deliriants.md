# Assignment 29 — Deliriants / atypical hallucinogens

Game catalog research for Bellhaven / TrapRaiders. **Not a cookbook.**

- No recipes, plant-prep how-to, doses, or extraction.
- Route notes are flags (`oral`, `smoke`, `tea`, `mislabel`).
- No opioids. Ibogaine has a kappa-ish / oneirogenic reputation — file it as **atypical**, not as an opioid analog project.
- Scores are **0–100 or unknown**. Unknown is `null`, never `0`.
- Lead with **rush / euphoria / compulsiveness / enjoyment**. Then the bill.

**Class rule:** delirium is **not** pretty psychedelic visuals. Do not score Datura or DPH like LSD or 2C-B. Realistic false people, no insight, no comedy overlay, no “the walls breathed nicely.” If an NPC says it was beautiful, they are lying or they took something else.

**Salvia is not a rush. It is a kidnapping.** Fast onset is salience, not pleasure.

DXM lives in assignment 17. 5-MeO-DMT / DMT live in 21–23. Nitrous in 17. Do not steal them.

# Anticholinergic deliriants

## Datura (jimsonweed, thorn apple, devil’s trumpet)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: **The plant that invents people. Not a trip. A lost weekend.**

### The hit
Rush `null (A)` — Oral / tea: nothing like a hit. Dry, then gone.  
Euphoria **6 (A)** — A handful of old “witchcraft” notes. The living file is dysphoria.  
Enjoyment **8 (A)** — Lowest fun that still has a huge report pile. People do not recommend it. They warn.  
Compulsiveness **8 (A)** — Almost nobody chases it. A few teenagers repeat once because they did not believe the first story.

### The bill
True delirium: conversations with people who are not there, cigarettes that are not there, spiders, police, family. **No insight** — they do not know they are high. Amnesia. Days of dilated pupils, dry as bone, fever, urinary retention, tachycardia. Hospital series are the literature. Deaths happen. This is not “strong LSD.” `freakout` `OD` `oral`.

NPC line: *"You will argue with a hallway. The hallway will win."*

Sources: Erowid Datura vault (overwhelmingly negative); CDC / poison-center jimsonweed series; tropane alkaloid reviews.  
Gaps: Alkaloid ratio varies by plant / season — identity is the plant, not a milligram. Do not write a prep card.

## Diphenhydramine (DPH, Benadryl)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: **Corner-store delirium. Shadow people. Compulsive in the worst way.**

### The hit
Rush `null (A)` — Oral: heavy body, restless legs, then the room populates.  
Euphoria **12 (A)** — A minority describe a warm dumb window. Majority: dysphoria from the first hour.  
Enjoyment **14 (A)** — “Never again” is the genre. A teen-loop exists anyway.

NPC line: *"The hat man is not a visual. He’s a roommate you invented."*

Sources: Erowid DPH vault; PsychonautWiki DPH; poison-center diphenhydramine abuse series; assignment 17 collision note.  
Gaps: Some Unisom SKUs are doxylamine — `mislabel` inside the pharmacy aisle.

# Atypical (not antimuscarinic — do not score as delirium *or* as acid)

## Salvia divinorum (salvinorin A)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: **Not a rush. A kidnapping. Kappa, not cannabis, not acid.**

### The hit
Rush **12 (A)** — Smoked onset is a **slam into elsewhere**. That is speed of onset, not pleasure. Do not file next to cocaine. Oral/quid is slower and still not a rush.  
Euphoria **18 (A)** — Rare “pleasant.” Common: being folded, becoming an object, meeting entities that do not care.  
Enjoyment **22 (A)** — Split: a few spiritual repeaters; the majority one-and-done. Retrospective “glad I know” ≠ enjoyment.  
Compulsiveness **8 (A)** — People do not empty the bag for fun. A stubborn subset does “one more to understand.” Low.

### The bill
Reality replacement for minutes that feel like years. Dysphoria, fear, injury from walking. Afterglow can be gentle or shaky. **Not pretty psychedelic visuals** — the world is replaced, not decorated. Not a deliriant (they usually *know* something impossible is happening, then they don’t have a self left to know). `smoke` `freakout`.

NPC line: *"You don’t get high. You get taken."*

Sources: Erowid Salvia vault; Siebert / Roth kappa work; PsychonautWiki salvinorin A; DEA / UN notes on salvia.  
Gaps: Fortified “extract” labels are a lottery — still one SKU (salvinorin A). Salvinorin B ethoxymethyl ether etc. are literature-only — names, not a wall.

## Muscimol / Amanita muscaria (fly agaric)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: **Drunk dream, not a mushroom trip. GABA, not psilocybin.**

### The hit
Rush `null (A)` — Slow, nauseous climb.  
Euphoria **34 (B)** — Warm, silly, dissociated comfort for the people who like it.  
Enjoyment **38 (B)** — Split: cozy / mythic vs vomit and delirium-adjacent confusion. Not 4-AcO-DMT.  
Compulsiveness **16 (B)** — Seasonal curiosity, not a binge demon.

### The bill
Nausea is the tax. Muscle twitch, sweats, sleep that is not sleep, confusion that can look delirious. Ibotenic acid is in the same mushroom — do not print a second recreational bottle. `oral` `freakout`. 2020s gummy market: `mislabel` (strength lottery, sometimes other drugs).

NPC line: *"Santa’s mushroom. The hangover is the folklore."*

Sources: Erowid Amanita; EUDA / national warnings on muscimol gummies (2020s); muscimol GABA reviews.  
Gaps: Shop gummies ≠ a Siberian story.

## Nutmeg (myristicin / *Myristica*)

Status: well_documented  
Market: street  
Headline: **Eight hours of waiting. Then a dirty, dry, never-again.**

### The hit
Rush `null (A)` — Nothing for a long time. Then a truck.  
Euphoria **16 (A)** — A few “stoned / MDMA-ish” letters from people who had not met either.  
Enjoyment **12 (A)** — The internet dare. The review is regret.  
Compulsiveness **10 (A)** — Almost no second date.

### The bill

NPC line: *"The spice rack dare. The rack wins."*

Sources: Erowid nutmeg vault; poison-center nutmeg series.  
Gaps: Myristicin vs elemicin vs leftover terpenes — score the pantry product.

# Names-only / do not ship as extra souls

- **Doxylamine, cyclizine, hydroxyzine, orphenadrine** — anticholinergic pharmacy leftovers. Thin recreational files. Dusty labels.
- **Hyoscine butylbromide (Buscopan)** — mostly doesn’t want to be a brain drug. Don’t invent a high.
- **Salvinorin B, 2-ethoxy-methyl-salvinorin B, herkinorin** — literature kappa tools. No bag chorus.
- **Muscazone, muscarine** — mushroom constituents. Muscarine is a poison story, not a party.
- **Elemicin, safrole** — nutmeg-adjacent names. No extra SKUs. **No precursor talk.**
- **Voacangine, tabernanthine** — iboga cousins. Names only. No analog project.
- **Blue lotus / aporphines** — mild shop ethnogen; 30 can list as a proposed leftover, not a deliriant.

**Out:** DXM (17), diphenidine (17), tryptamines (21–23), lysergamides (24), cannabis / spice (27–28), opioids / kratom / tianeptine (banned).
